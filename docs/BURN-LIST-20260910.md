# THE BURN LIST — what the 60-day story built, and what happens to it

Ops' call (Mike, 2026-09-10: "Burn list: Ops call. Work with me on what I
need to deliver, then on how to deliver, and deal with the tools and
processes behind the scenes."). Drafted 2026-09-10, executed in week 2
(the calendar's "Burn-down executed", 09-26). Three verdicts:

- **keep** — it serves the infomercial wing, the albums, the reels, or the
  running of the week as it is;
- **re-cut** — the thing stays, its shape changes to the albums, the
  catalogue or the promotion;
- **burn** — archived whole under `docs/archive/` (nothing is deleted from
  history) and removed from the working tree, the desk and the scripts.

Rule: a burn is a move to the archive with a one-line README entry, in one
commit per group, gates green after each. Mike's words are never burned;
where a burned document holds his words, they are quoted into
`docs/canon/11-STANDING-RULINGS.md` first.
