# THE MANUAL'S FACTS SITTING — every ruling the manual needs, in one place

**For Mike. Written 2026-09-02, Ops.** Under his ruling A on the manual (Ops drafts whole sections from the canon; Mike marks up and approves by section), this is the second step after the register test: the list of facts the manual cannot be written around. Each is a question the canon holds two or more answers to, or none. Answer by number and letter, in any order, at any pace; a question left unanswered is written around, and the brief says how. Nothing here is a design; every line traces to `docs/canon/CONFLICTS.md` or `docs/canon/HOLES.md`.

**Two rules that shape every answer here.** The manual is a 1965 document; the real firmware is not evidence about it (Doctrine 18), so "the hardware says" is not an argument, only a fact about a different object. And a published page cannot be unsaid: three positions (7-19, SP 7-14, B-1) are on the glass already and some options below are foreclosed by them.

- Recommended: **A**.

**3. How many lamps: one multi-colour lamp or two?** (K-09) Firmware has two pixels; the 2024 manual says one. This is the named instance in Doctrine 18.

- Recommended: **C** ("no counts" already points there).

**4. The cell's numbers: capacity, voltage, chemistry, charge time, run time, mass and dimensions of the instrument, temperature limits.** (H-02) None exist; the bench never measured them. 7-19 is published without them.
- **A.** Measure the physical unit (mass, dimensions) and invent the electrical figures as period fiction, in 1965 units and 1965 confidence.
