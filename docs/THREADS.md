# LOOSE THREADS — canon that has not been said yet

Things that are **true in the story** and have not appeared in a Record. They
live here so they get woven in rather than lost.

## THE RULES

- **A thread is canon the moment it is written down**, whether or not a Record
  has said it yet. It is not a draft and it is not a maybe.
- **Ops never rewrites a thread.** Mike's words go in as typed — the same rule
  the Record's own dictation runs on.
- **When a Record uses a thread, mark it WOVEN and name the Record.** It stays
  on this page: a used thread is still canon, and a later Record must not
  contradict it.
- **A LOOSE thread is not a task.** Nobody has to use it. Nothing counts them,
  nothing is late, and a thread that is never woven has not failed.

## WHERE THREAD-002 CAME FROM

It is **week one's own headline, in Mike's words**, written on 2026-08-07 into
the twelve-week arc (`ARC.W1`) and into the worksheet as `W1.SUM`. Week one has
since been written — **Records 001 to 005** — and **the headline landed in none
of them**: measured against `reveal/record-entries.mjs`, no entry contains the
phrase. It is on `arc.html` and `reference.html`, which are Ops instruments,
and nowhere a visitor can read.

It is here rather than closed because **the week it names is already on the
glass**. Everything else in the twelve-week arc is a plan for a week that has
not happened; this is a sentence about a week that has.

## WHAT THE SEARCH FOUND, AND WHAT IT DID NOT

Searched for canon-but-unpublished material: every round log in `docs/`, the
handoffs, the dictation instruments and their rescued browser stores, the magnet
pile, `answers.json`, and `robots-record.js`.

**Two threads, and only two.** The rest of what a search turns up is not canon:

- **The logs are almost entirely PROCESS.** Every `MIKE: "..."` quote in them is
  a ruling about the museum — hide this, capitalise that, the lines are too
  long. A ruling is not a fact in the story.
- **The magnet pile holds no text at all** — two tiles, both position-and-id
  only.
- **Eleven of the thirteen answered worksheet slots are already in Records 001
  to 005 character for character**, which is what the 2026-08-09 measurement
  found and what makes the twelfth worth noticing.
- **The other eleven weeks of the arc are plans**, not canon: they describe
  weeks that have not happened.

**Nothing was inferred and nothing was reworded.** A sentence that had to be
interpreted to become a thread is not a thread.
