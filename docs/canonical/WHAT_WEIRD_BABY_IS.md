# WHAT WEIRD.BABY IS — Mike's words

**Standing statement, filed 2026-09-02.** On Mike's ruling (option A, 2026-09-02) the April vision documents (`VISION.md`, `VISION_LOCK_v0.3.md`, the UX specs) moved to `docs/archive/` as history. Until Mike writes the paragraph that replaces them, the lines below are the statement of what Weird.Baby is. Every quoted line is his, verbatim, casing and punctuation as he gave it. The headings are Ops'. Nothing here is a design; it is the test a design is held against.

---

## The centre

> "Everything leads to me, which online is my entire digital ecosystem."

> "I'm like Disney. Is it the films, animations, parks, assets? It's Disney."

> "The website is not the goal. I am the goal."

> "The museum is a tool."

(2026-09-01 and 2026-09-02.) The music, the robots and the foundation each stand alone and each points back to him. The site is one room in the ecosystem, not the ecosystem and not the top of it.

## The five reasons the site exists

In his order (2026-09-01):

1. **A place to go from "weird.baby?" to "Weird.Baby!" in 60 seconds or less.**
2. **A playground to have fun immediately** — queue up tunes and vids, the PUV scroller auto-starts, check his stats.
3. **Sales** — gift shop merch and robot sales. **Music won't make money on the site.**
4. **Promote his music** — you heard one song, hear the rest.
5. **His philanthropic views and support for CFH.**

## The draw

> "People love reading headlines, not articles."

> "Most people are drawn to a Disney image yet few are going there any time soon."

The daily is the draw. The real content behind it is what makes the daily worth arriving for. Remove the real thing and the draw goes with it.

## The lane

> "One lane: the workshop."

> "Weird.Baby is a brand like Disney — a place with characters in it."

## Who decides
