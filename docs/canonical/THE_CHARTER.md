## The Purpose — immutable
Weird.Baby exists to make the world demonstrably better, and it starts
with music: the artists who deserve depth, and the people who love them.
Everything else in this document protects that.

## The Law — no exceptions, no amendments, ever
1. **Free. Equally free. Always.** No tickets, no tiers, no ads, no
   paywalls. Rich people aren't special here. Every visitor is royalty.
2. **One person is Weird.Baby.** That person is Papa. Papa is paid
   nothing — not salary, not expenses, not credit taken. Ever.
3. **Weird.Baby owns nothing.** Everything it needs arrives as a gift:
   the design, the code, the shelf, the legal work. Gifts of service,
   never gifts of money. Money is not accepted — money is only passed on.
4. **Money never stops here.** What passes through Weird.Baby's hands
   goes to humanity — all of it, always, without pause and without pay.
5. **We take nothing that isn't ours.** Artists keep every penny of
   theirs. The museum never fights over the last piece of pie; it works
   to make more pie.
6. **Methods change. Purpose doesn't.** How things get done will be
   rewritten a thousand times. What they're done *for* is written once.

## Succession
When a Papa's keeping ends, one person — chosen, not elected; willing,
not paid — takes the responsibility on the same terms: own nothing,
take nothing, keep the place, pass it on. There is no committee, no
board, no team. There is a Papa, and then there is the next one.

## Dissolution
If humanity stops believing in what Weird.Baby is for, Weird.Baby does
not pivot, rebrand, or sell. It ends. The name dies with it — retired
forever, never to be used by anyone, for anything, protected as far as
protection is possible. Even that protection arrives the only way
anything ever arrives here: as someone's gift. What survives is the
idea. Someone else may begin again — with their own name.

## The Test
This charter is written in terms so plain and so human that to disagree
with it, you'd have to be an absolute turd.
