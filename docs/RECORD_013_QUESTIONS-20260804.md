- **Why it matters:** without it the register prints no date on the index row,

  "as it stood on this day".

## B · THE BAG AND THE ADAPTER

**B1.** What is the bag actually like?
- **Known:** it is sealed and it is modern.
- **Missing:** what kind of bag — clear film, anti-static, a ziplock, printed,
  labelled, sealed how.
- **Why it matters:** "sealed modern bag" is currently the whole description. A
  reader cannot picture it, and the bag is the reason the entry exists.

**B2.** Where in the delivery was it?
- **Known:** it was packed differently from everything else.
- **Missing:** which container, and where in it (taped to a wall, loose on top,
  under the object, in a corner).
- **Why it matters:** *where* it was placed is the difference between somebody
  hiding it and somebody making sure it was found.

**B3.** How was everything ELSE packed?
- **Known:** differently from the bag.
- **Missing:** the actual packing — material, era, condition.
- **Why it matters:** the entry's whole point is a contrast, and only one side
  of the contrast is currently on the page. (The removed draft said newspaper,
  tape and tan padding. Invented.)

**B4.** What does the adapter's other end look like?
- **Known:** it is a USB-C adapter.
- **Missing:** what the output end is — connector type, size, markings, printed
  voltage or amperage, brand, part number, or none of the above.
- **Why it matters:** it decides whether this reads as a modern off-the-shelf
  part or as something made for this machine.

**B5.** Was anything else in or on the bag?
- **Known:** nothing.
- **Missing:** note, label, receipt, part number — or a confirmed nothing.
- **Why it matters:** "there was nothing with it" is a real and printable fact,
  but only you can confirm it. Silence is not the same as nothing.
