LOOSE THREADS — canon that has not been said yet

Things that are true in the story and have not appeared in a Record. They live here so they get woven in rather than lost.

THE RULES

  • A thread is canon the moment it is written down, whether or not a Record has said it yet. It is not a draft and it is not a maybe.
  • Ops never rewrites a thread. Mike's words go in as typed — the same rule the Record's own dictation runs on.
  • When a Record uses a thread, mark it WOVEN and name the Record. It stays on this page: a used thread is still canon, and a later Record must not contradict it.
  • A LOOSE thread is not a task. Nobody has to use it. Nothing counts them, nothing is late, and a thread that is never woven has not failed.

WHERE THREAD-003 CAME FROM

It arrived on 2026-08-20, in writing, with Records 004 and 005 — the two entries that raise and approve Req 0628. The requisition is on the glass; the thread is what is behind it, and none of it is said in either Record.

The Quality man who declared the Portal unsafe to run was fired for it, without anyone ever saying so. Req 0628 is his backfill: promoted from inside, no new hires, and at the end of the chain the last seat quietly goes unfilled — no net increase in head count. The company removed an obstacle and saved a salary and no line item says either.

[2026-08-21] QC_101 IS PUBLISHED NOW AND IT IS NOT ABOUT THIS MAN. The form attached to Record 004 is dated 1965 and it PASSES — signed off with no marginalia at all, by an inspector whose monogram is illegible by design. The Quality man of THREAD-003 is the 2026 one, the voice behind Record 004's "QC RULE: Unsafe to run in any sandbox; permanently quarantined." Two men, sixty-one years apart, opposite verdicts, and the thread belongs to the second. Recorded here because the sheet is the obvious thing a later round would reach for, and reaching for it would put the wrong man in the story. Canon: 06-PORTAL §8.

Somebody moved up into a chair that emptied for a reason. That person exists and has not appeared.

Mike, on what it is for: "The story is over, but the seed exists to grow another egg, chapter, etc."

IT IS LOOSE AND IT IS ALREADY HALF-PUBLISHED, which is the thing to notice: Record 004 prints "Quality has declared it unsafe to run in any sandbox; permanently quarantined" and both entries print "No net increase in head count". A reader has every visible piece and none of the meaning. So this thread is not waiting to be introduced — it is waiting to be paid off, and a later Record contradicting it would contradict something already on the wall.

WHERE THREAD-002 CAME FROM

It is week one's own headline, in Mike's words, written on 2026-08-07 into the twelve-week arc (ARC.W1) and into the worksheet as W1.SUM. Week one has since been written — Records 001 to 005 — and the headline landed in none of them: measured against reveal/record-entries.mjs, no entry contains the phrase. It is on arc.html and reference.html, which are Ops instruments, and nowhere a visitor can read.

It is here rather than closed because the week it names is already on the glass. Everything else in the twelve-week arc is a plan for a week that has not happened; this is a sentence about a week that has.

Nothing was inferred and nothing was reworded. A sentence that had to be interpreted to become a thread is not a thread.
