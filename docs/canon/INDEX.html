THE CANON — index

One catalogue of everything this world holds. Written so Ops can answer a specific question fast, not so anybody reads it through.

IT RESOLVES NOTHING. Every conflict is Mike's to rule. Where two accounts disagree they are both recorded, with their sources, and neither is marked likely. A catalogue that quietly picked winners is worse than no catalogue, because nobody can tell which entries were decided and which were found.

HOW THIS CATALOGUE IS BUILT

Read this before adding to it.

THE ONE RULE

OPS RESOLVES NOTHING. Every conflict is Mike's to rule. If you find yourself deciding which of two accounts is true, STOP and record both with their sources.

THE FOUR FIELDS EVERY ENTRY CARRIES

fieldvalues
What it meansas attested. Where the corpus names a thing and never says what it is, the row says so and stops.
Sourcefile and, where it matters, line
RegisterSTORY — the machine or its documents say it · OPS — we say it about the machine and it has never been on the glass or on the page. Doctrine 18 turns on that difference and the two must never be mixed into one appendix.
PublicationPUB / SCHEDULED / unmarked — see 09-PUBLISHED

Plus, where it applies: contested (K-nn) and absent (H-nn).

THE THREE RULES HELD THROUGHOUT

  1. NO TERM IS INVENTED. Every row is attested and the row names where.
  2. NO CONFLICT IS RESOLVED. No account is marked likely, preferred or better sourced. Where a document itself claims priority, that claim is quoted as a fact about the document — it is not this catalogue agreeing with it.
  3. A HOLE IS ABSENT, NOT ZERO. "The cell has no stated capacity" is a hole; "the cell has no capacity" would be a fact, and a false one.

WHERE OPS' OWN JUDGEMENT APPEARS, IT SAYS SO

Three places, and they are marked in the text:

  • the reading of the four failure modes (FAILURE-MODES §1);
  • the cost estimates for each (FAILURE-MODES §3);
  • one observation about 6.28 (09-PUBLISHED), explicitly labelled not canon.

Nothing else on these pages is Ops' opinion.

WHAT IS NOT IN HERE

Named so nobody assumes it was covered and found nothing.

  • The Weird.Baby music wing (/wal, Hunter Root, the Foundation, the gift shop). A different world in the same museum. Nothing about the machines lives there.
  • The burp library, the storyboards, the video rounds — production material in robots:content/ and robots:docs/BURP_*, MAGIC8_*. Swept for facts about the world and found to hold production process, not canon. If a fact about this world is in there, it is not in here, and that is the largest known gap.
  • The 89-folder 2024 firmware history and the 11 OneDrive originals. Indexed in LINEAGE.md, read through the MKR extraction summarised in SPEC_INVENTORY. The originals themselves were not re-read this round.
  • Word-level content — the persona answer grids cell by cell, the fortune cookies, the nicknames. The tables are named and described; their contents are in the CSVs.
