THE 2021 NARRATION IS A DATED PRIMARY SOURCE

Ruling, Mike, 2026-08-03. IMG_1526.MOV (2021-03-19) is excellent evidence of what Mike was building in March 2021. It is not a description of any machine that exists now.

A 2021 sentence may be quoted as a 2021 sentence, dated and attributed. It may never be quoted as a 2026 fact. If a claim about the current machine rests only on the 2021 audio, the claim is unsupported. If you find a conflict, the 2021 tape is the one that loses.

THE REEL IS OUT OF THE DESIGN ENTIRELY

Mike, 2026-08-05: "THE REEL IS NO LONGER PART OF THE DESIGN. Remove it from the design record and show it nowhere."

The 60-stop electromechanical pinball credit reel in the 2021 video is not in the current build and is not the plan.

AND THE HALF THAT USED TO SURVIVE IS ALSO STRUCK. LINEAGE previously kept the claim that the control room's drum descends from the wheel as an idiom and that "the idea is canon." That clause is struck. There is no surviving ancestry claim, no idiom, and no lineage from the credit reel to anything.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT STRIKE: the Portal's drum. That is a live built control on the museum's glass and it stays. What is gone is the claim that it descends from the reel. A drum is a drum; it does not need a grandfather.

6 · THE FLAGSHIP'S DECLARED RULES

PUBLISHED, on the MGK-NIAC album's Technical Specifications face:

MATRIX   8 × 16 — seven rows visible, the eighth wired, driven and behind something
BAR      1 × 64, addressed as a single chain
OUTPUTS  2 matrix chains · 2 bar chains · 3 servos
DECLARED five rules
RULE 1   a numerical envelope
RULE 2   a ceiling of eight core states
RULE 3   mutual exclusion
RULE 4   a reveal no faster than twelve seconds
RULE 5   no adaptive learning — the machine is forbidden, in writing, from getting to know you

RULE 5 IS THE ONE THAT CARRIES WEIGHT ELSEWHERE. The learning-machine reading — the device secretly LEARNING its owner to optimise control — is HELD as a reading and never forced (STORY_BIBLE.html), and Rule 5 is the machine's own written denial of it. The two are not reconciled anywhere. Recorded, not resolved.

7 · THE TWIN

What it isPUB, in the museum's own words: "It is the real firmware on shimmed hardware — the twin. The unit itself is a physical object in a room; the twin is how it is met from here."
The doctrine"A twin of the BASE UNIT goes freely accessible online, played as the real thing. In-fiction frame: you are running the original vintage software used to program and test MGK-VIIIp units." Free forever, museum-exhibit grade.
The frameThe security-monitor frame (canon, A2): the twin presents as a B&W security-monitor feed of an excavation — someone hacked back to the source servers where every personality was last backed up, and the viewer watches through a camera pointed at running equipment. Data resolves as more is excavated. Control of the camera is eventually gained.
The ruleThe twin is the dev environment; the unit is the print run. Physical unit outranks extraction. Single-file, no-network is LAW.
Whererobots:tools/viiip_twin.html — 10,802 lines. Served in the museum at /held/robots/twin.html, behind the stage door.
HoleIt has no in-fiction home of any kind — H-23

IT IS NOT AN EMULATION OF A DESCRIPTION. Whole firmware modules are carried into it verbatim and marked — [BIAS:29-55] exact, [FLASH:11-22] exact — RAM-only, [3_PROC_MGR.ino - exact], 2_data_MENU.ino parsed from source. Save_To_Flash is a stub in the twin because it is a stub in the firmware.

THE REFERENCE PHOTOGRAPHS ARE HARDWARE REFERENCE ONLY — four of them: front screen, front full, rear power switch, top monitor. The firmware and screens visible in them are OUTDATED (old code, months to years). Never match twin behaviour to them.
