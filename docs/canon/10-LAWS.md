# 10 · THE LAWS — story laws and Ops doctrines

**TWO KINDS AND THEY MUST NOT BE MIXED.**

- **STORY LAWS** govern what is true in the world and what the machine may be
  made to say. Breaking one is a continuity error.
- **OPS DOCTRINES** govern how the museum and the repos are worked. They are
  `OPS` register by definition. **None of them may ever reach a visitor.**

**The single most consequential rule for anybody reading this catalogue is
[Doctrine 18](#doctrine-18): roughly a third of this catalogue is OPS register
and must never reach a page.**

# PART TWO · THE OPS DOCTRINES

**`OPS` register throughout. Canonical text: `museum:docs/canonical/OPERATIONS_ARCHIVE/OPERATIONS-FULL-b9517e6.md`
§7 (the manual was collapsed 2026-09-01). Numbered as they are numbered there.**

## Doctrine 11 · THE LAW OF THE VISIBLE LINE
<a id="doctrine-11"></a>

> **If a line describes the work rather than doing the work, it does not ship.**

**THE TEST IS THE LINE'S SUBJECT, not its tone or its truth.**

**FAILS:** the drafting, the research, the revision history, what a round did,
the form a page takes and why, the typography, the renderers, unwritten-content
plans, any builder named on the glass, internal decision codes, `· v1` draft
stamps.

**SHIPS:** the objects, the artists, the events in the record, the standing
terms, **and honest statements of what is not held.** *"No plate on file"*
ships; *"nobody has photographed this yet"* does not.

**TWO THINGS THAT LOOK LIKE META AND ARE NOT:** **provenance** (sources lines,
accession numbers) and **mechanism state** (LIVE / NOT BUILT).

**COROLLARY: empty and honest beats populated and false.**

**THE PRACTICAL TRAP:** the `why` on a Portal drum position, the `held` on a
Record door, and a `note` on a face entry are all **PRINTED**. *A comment-shaped
string in a data file is not a comment.*

## Doctrine 12 · OPS DOES NOT INVENT CONTENT
<a id="doctrine-12"></a>

**The reason `SER. NO.` and `DATE` are blank on the FEED CONTROL's badge
([H-21](HOLES.md#h-21)), the reason the NIAC drum positions carry a name and
nothing else, and the reason the reveal ledger's `when` is null on all 174 rows.**

**A plausible invented specific is the exact failure this doctrine names.**

## Doctrine 16 · THE LAW OF SUBTRACTION
<a id="doctrine-16"></a>

> **If it does not help, it hurts. If it does not need to be there, it needs to
> not be there.**

**IT IS NOT DOCTRINE 11 AGAIN.** 11 tests a line's **SUBJECT**; this tests its
**NECESSITY**. Ask of any object, control, count, caption or badge: **what is
lost if it goes?** *"Nothing a reader would miss"* means it goes. **Harmless is
not passing — harmless costs attention.**

**It is a reason to delete, never a reason to delete QUIETLY.**

## Doctrine 18 · IN-STORY TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
<a id="doctrine-18"></a>

> **"Technical Specifications" means THE IN-STORY SPECS, NEVER THE REAL ONES.**

**Given as a global standard with Mike's own forecast attached:** ***"this drifts
back easily."***

**WHAT A SPECIFICATION SURFACE IS.** Any surface whose subject is *what the
machine is.* **Defined by SUBJECT, not by heading** — a face that starts listing
the machine's components becomes one the moment it does.

**WHAT MAY NOT BE ON ONE:** the board's part number, source-tree filenames, the
module list, the bench sketch count, the count of lines of source, the I²C
addresses, the pin numbers, the validation state of the workshop, **and any
calendar date of the real build.** *These are all TRUE. They are the provenance
of a prop, and a spec sheet is not a provenance record.*

**WHAT IS NOT COVERED:** **the Record is the house's log of receiving the object
THIS YEAR** — a sealed modern bag, a USB-C adapter, a slow charge. All modern,
all correct, on a surface whose subject is *now.* **The rule is about which
machine a spec sheet is describing, not about banning the present tense.**

**WHY IT DRIFTS BACK, WHICH IS THE PART WORTH KNOWING:** *the real facts are the
ones Ops can VERIFY. A session looking for something true to put on a spec sheet
reaches for the firmware tree every time, because the firmware tree is the thing
it can read — and it will feel like diligence while it does it.* **Recording the
law does not stop it; a gate does** — `npm run instory:gate`.

**AND IT BEARS ON TWO CONFLICTS IN THIS CATALOGUE**, in a direction that is easy
to miss: [K-08](CONFLICTS.md#k-08) and [K-09](CONFLICTS.md#k-09). *"The in-story
manual is the authority for the in-story spec, and **the real firmware is not
evidence about a 1965 machine at all.**"* Register row **N-i**.

## Doctrine 21 · EVERYTHING IN THE FORM IS STORY
<a id="doctrine-21"></a>

**Material arriving through the dictation instruments is in-story by
construction**, so Doctrine 11 is satisfied **at the instrument** and no future
dictation gets re-adjudicated entry by entry.

**IT DOES NOT EXEMPT OPS' OWN PROSE ANYWHERE**, including inside a Record entry,
**and it does not lower the provenance bar.**

## Doctrine 22 · A LIMIT IS SHOWN WHERE THE STRING IS WRITTEN
<a id="doctrine-22"></a>

> **He must never again discover a limit from a report.**

**A constraint has three halves: a render that cannot lie, a gate that refuses,
and a live count on the field.** **WARN, NEVER BLOCK.** Count what would be
SAVED, not the raw field. **Say where there is NO limit.** The warning travels
into the paste.

## Doctrine 23 · THE RECORD'S REGISTER — EMAIL-LIKE, NOT AN EMAIL PROGRAM
<a id="doctrine-23"></a>

**No From, no To, no Subject line, no reply affordances, no inbox, no message
headers, no envelope furniture of any kind.** What is borrowed is **the REGISTER
ONLY**: the plainness and the attachments-at-the-bottom convention.

**THE TEST IS NOT *is it useful* — IT IS *would a mail program have it*.**

## Doctrine 24 · ONCE IT IS RULED GONE, IT IS GONE FROM HIS VIEW
<a id="doctrine-24"></a>

**Not archived where it resurfaces, not listed in a tracker, not carried in a
register as a closed row he has to scroll past.** **A deleted thing is named
ONCE, in the log of the round that killed it, and nowhere else.**

**THE TEST IS NOT *is it archived*, IT IS *will he meet it again*.**

**THIS CATALOGUE IS BOUND BY IT.** Nothing on these pages is a resurrection of
something Mike ruled gone. Where a struck thing is named — the credit reel, THE
MORGUE, the `_tmp/` line, `MGK-65x` — **it is named once, in the row that
records the striking, because a catalogue that does not record a strike invites
the next session to rebuild it.**

## Doctrine 25 · THE TOOLS ARE FOR WORKING, NOT FOR BRIEFING
<a id="doctrine-25"></a>

> *"All the stuff at the top, I never read."*

**Construction clause: on the field, in the footer, or on a reference page —
never above the work.** **A LEGEND IS NOT A BRIEFING** — the test is whether the
page is unreadable without it.

## Doctrine 26 · LEAD WITH WHAT HE MUST DO OR DECIDE
<a id="doctrine-26"></a>

**Every report to Mike opens with what is waiting on him.** **THE TEST IS NOT *is
it true* AND NOT *is it interesting* — IT IS *does this change what he does
next?*** **AN EMPTY ASK IS A COMPLETE REPORT.**

## THE PROVENANCE GATE
<a id="provenance"></a>

**`npm run provenance:gate` runs beside lint and build on every packet.** It
fails if any visitor-facing string in `src/` or `index.html` has no row in
`provenance/register.json`, or any referenced image has no row in
`provenance/assets.json`.

**Classes:** `MIKE` · `VERIFIED` · `DERIVED` · `HOUSE` · `RESTATED`.

**APPROVAL IS NOT AUTHORSHIP.** A sentence Mike approved that Ops wrote is filed
**RESTATED**, not MIKE, **and must never be re-marked** — *a paraphrase in his
class is indistinguishable from his words a week later.*

**AND THE GATE CANNOT VERIFY THAT A DECLARATION IS TRUE.** That is the largest
hole in it and it is written down in `provenance/README.md` §4.

## THE THREE MARKS
<a id="three-marks"></a>

**In the dictation instruments, and the rule that governs them.**

| mark | meaning |
|---|---|
| `Ops` **blue** | Ops' sentence |
| **`your words` gold** | **VERBATIM** |
| `your rule · Ops wording` **amber** | his rule, Ops' sentence |

**A `beat` MAY BE DELETED BUT NEVER REWORDED.**

**BOTH ERRORS COST THE SAME:** *a paraphrase in gold is indistinguishable a week
later from something he said, and his own sentence left in blue gets quietly
"improved" by the next round.*

**OPS DIVIDES AND DOES NOT INVENT.**
