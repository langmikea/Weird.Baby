## 5 · THE THREE LAYERS
<a id="three-layers"></a>

**Structural law. `THE_STORY_ARC.md`, CANON v1.0. It governs every word written
into the machine.**

1. **Downstairs — honest machinery.** The machine has **no self-awareness and
   never convincingly pretends otherwise.** It performs calculations against
   prescribed probability models. **It does not think. Ever.**
2. **In between — staged theatre.** Personalities are **obviously staged**,
   1965-style: *"IT TALKS!"*, *"REAL LIGHTS!"* Window dressing proudly worn,
   seams visible. **Acts, not minds. Committed, in-character, nobody home.**
3. **Upstairs — the unknown wire.** The machine connects to servers that appear
   fully autonomous — scheduled syncs, batch filings, service routines running on
   sixty years of momentum. **WHO OR WHAT is on the other end is DELIBERATELY
   UNANSWERED.** Canon never confirms autonomy, never denies presence.
   **Writing discipline: passive voice, procedural language, no signatures.
   Consistent with autonomy; never proof of it.**

**AMENDMENT 2 SHARPENS LAYER 3 AND DOES NOT REPLACE IT:** **NOBODY is at
headquarters.** The system runs autonomously — always has, always will. This does
not preclude that **someone else hacked in exactly as we did** — and perhaps
stalled because **they didn't have the units.** The other presence is a **rival
excavator, not an operator.** Good guys / bad guys: **deliberately unresolved** —
the recovery team itself may be either.

**Consequence for [H-03](HOLES.md#h-03):** the identity of the far end is a
**sealed** hole, not an open one.

## 7 · THE LAWS OF THE WORLD

These live in full at [LAWS](10-LAWS.md). Named here because they are facts about
the world rather than about the build:

- **THE NEVER-ADVERTISED LAW** — the chapters are never pitched; **discovery of
  the story's existence is part of the story.**
- **THE EXPERIENCE LAW** — **THERE IS NO GAME.** No win state, no right way. *"The
  experience is finding a 50-60-year-old smartphone that still boots and snooping
  — because it's COOL."*
- **THE SCOPE LAW** — the story is a **jumbo Easter egg, not the product.**
- **THE FORMAT LAW** — **NO inter-persona communication, ever.** All messages
  originate from virtual servers; users become aware of counterparts, never able
  to reach them.
- **THE CLEAN-EXIT LAW** — **only the Housewife exits clean.** Everyone else is
  already defective or damaged by the process; the CEO may be innocent and still
  not clean.
- **STORY FREEZE (2026-07-22)** — bugs are fixable opportunistically; **the story
  never changes post-ship — or WB pays all upgrade costs.**
