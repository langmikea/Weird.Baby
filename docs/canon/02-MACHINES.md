### THE 2021 NARRATION IS A DATED PRIMARY SOURCE
<a id="the-2021-rule"></a>

**Ruling, Mike, 2026-08-03.** `IMG_1526.MOV` (2021-03-19) is excellent evidence
of **what Mike was building in March 2021.** It is not a description of any
machine that exists now.

> **A 2021 sentence may be quoted as a 2021 sentence, dated and attributed. It
> may never be quoted as a 2026 fact.** If a claim about the current machine
> rests only on the 2021 audio, the claim is unsupported. **If you find a
> conflict, the 2021 tape is the one that loses.**

### THE REEL IS OUT OF THE DESIGN ENTIRELY
<a id="the-reel"></a>

**Mike, 2026-08-05:** ***"THE REEL IS NO LONGER PART OF THE DESIGN. Remove it
from the design record and show it nowhere."***

The 60-stop electromechanical pinball credit reel in the 2021 video is **not in
the current build and is not the plan.**

**AND THE HALF THAT USED TO SURVIVE IS ALSO STRUCK.** LINEAGE previously kept the
claim that the control room's drum descends from the wheel as an **idiom** and
that *"the idea is canon."* **That clause is struck. There is no surviving
ancestry claim, no idiom, and no lineage from the credit reel to anything.**

**WHAT THIS DOES NOT STRIKE:** the Portal's drum. That is a live built control on
the museum's glass and it stays. What is gone is **the claim that it descends
from the reel.** *A drum is a drum; it does not need a grandfather.*
