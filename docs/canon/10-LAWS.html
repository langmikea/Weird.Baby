10 · THE LAWS — story laws and Ops doctrines

TWO KINDS AND THEY MUST NOT BE MIXED.

  • STORY LAWS govern what is true in the world and what the machine may be made to say. Breaking one is a continuity error.
  • OPS DOCTRINES govern how the museum and the repos are worked. They are OPS register by definition. None of them may ever reach a visitor.

The single most consequential rule for anybody reading this catalogue is Doctrine 18: roughly a third of this catalogue is OPS register and must never reach a page.

PART ONE · THE STORY LAWS

THE THREE LAYERS

See 01-WORLD §5. Honest machinery / obviously-staged 1965 theatre / the unknown wire. On conflict, the laws win.

STORY FREEZE

Frozen as of 2026-07-22. Bugs are fixable opportunistically; the story never changes post-ship — or WB pays all upgrade costs.

THE NEVER-ADVERTISED LAW

The chapters are NOT the reason to own the device and are NEVER pitched. The device earns its keep as a genuinely fun useless thing. Discovery of the story's EXISTENCE is part of the story. The deep game must never leak into the shallow one.

THE EXPERIENCE LAW

THERE IS NO GAME. No win state, no right way. "The experience is finding a 50-60-year-old smartphone that still boots and snooping — because it's COOL."

THE SCOPE LAW

The story is a jumbo Easter egg, not the product.

THE FORMAT LAW

NO inter-persona communication, ever. All messages originate from virtual servers; users become aware of counterparts, never able to reach them.

THE CLEAN-EXIT LAW

Only the Housewife exits clean. Everyone else is already defective or damaged by the process; the CEO may be innocent and still not clean.

SILENCE MEANS GENERIC

Everything is generic unless explicitly persona-reserved. All owner messages are persona-reserved.

THE DRAFTING LAW

Locked after a violation was caught.

Claude-as-the-computer assembles from established canon ONLY — no coined catchphrases, signatures, gags, or terminology. ALL flavour is Papa's.

It is the rule this catalogue is built under, and the reason H-13 flags determination despite it being a good word.

MILESTONE 1 CANON, CONSTITUTIONAL, 2026-07-24.

Users never receive every version of every tool at once. Capability parcels out in accordance with a story that at least justifies the reason. The slow-walk discovery reveal is the product's SHAPE, fleet-wide, forever.

Existing machinery — download-more, delayed/errored/counter discoveries, earned eggs, code ceremony, the casino gate — is the same law, now named.

THE SITE-WIDE B&W LAW (Mike, 2026-08-04): "EVERYTHING is B&W except a few colour Polaroid-quality family shots." It does not amend Article 4; it is what Article 4 is FOR. Together: colour at capture, B&W at delivery. The colour render must stay one flag away, and that flag kept working.

THE ROOM IS NOT THE MACHINE, as amended:

THE POSTERS ARE FAIR GAME. THE FACE IS NOT. Poster art is art, and it is his own wall. An identifiable PHOTOGRAPHIC PORTRAIT of a real person stays OUT of anything published — "A real face is not ours to publish."

The distinction is photographic likeness, not subject matter. Wall, floor and window are unchanged, and privacy wins every tie.

B&W DOES NOT CURE A PORTRAIT.

RE-ENCODE, ALWAYS. The 2021 source files carry a GPS tag — a home address to ~65 m. A source .MOV or camera-original .JPG is never published, under any circumstance, as any asset class. Strip metadata explicitly.

THE ONE SCOPE RULING: "THE CROPS ARE TOO DEEP — SHOW THE WHOLE ROBOT." The 2021 Magic 8 footage is carved out — "this footage is Mike's own, of his own build, and he wants it seen." It does not repeal Article 1 for anything the museum publishes of a unit. Article 3 is the live question there and is a per-asset call Mike has not made.

REVIEW CUTS ARE EXEMPT, ON TWO MANDATORY CONDITIONS: delivered outside any published path, and marked as a review cut with a per-shot statement of which articles it would fail. A review cut is never promoted by being copied — it is re-derived under the law or it does not ship.

PART TWO · THE OPS DOCTRINES

OPS register throughout. Canonical text: museum:docs/canonical/OPERATIONS_ARCHIVE/OPERATIONS-FULL-b9517e6.md §7 (the manual was collapsed 2026-09-01). Numbered as they are numbered there.

Doctrine 11 · THE LAW OF THE VISIBLE LINE

If a line describes the work rather than doing the work, it does not ship.

THE TEST IS THE LINE'S SUBJECT, not its tone or its truth.

FAILS: the drafting, the research, the revision history, what a round did, the form a page takes and why, the typography, the renderers, unwritten-content plans, any builder named on the glass, internal decision codes, · v1 draft stamps.

SHIPS: the objects, the artists, the events in the record, the standing terms, and honest statements of what is not held. "No plate on file" ships; "nobody has photographed this yet" does not.

TWO THINGS THAT LOOK LIKE META AND ARE NOT: provenance (sources lines, accession numbers) and mechanism state (LIVE / NOT BUILT).

COROLLARY: empty and honest beats populated and false.

THE PRACTICAL TRAP: the why on a Portal drum position, the held on a Record door, and a note on a face entry are all PRINTED. A comment-shaped string in a data file is not a comment.

Doctrine 12 · OPS DOES NOT INVENT CONTENT

The reason SER. NO. and DATE are blank on the FEED CONTROL's badge (H-21), the reason the NIAC drum positions carry a name and nothing else, and the reason the reveal ledger's when is null on all 174 rows.

A plausible invented specific is the exact failure this doctrine names.

Doctrine 16 · THE LAW OF SUBTRACTION

If it does not help, it hurts. If it does not need to be there, it needs to not be there.

IT IS NOT DOCTRINE 11 AGAIN. 11 tests a line's SUBJECT; this tests its NECESSITY. Ask of any object, control, count, caption or badge: what is lost if it goes? "Nothing a reader would miss" means it goes. Harmless is not passing — harmless costs attention.

It is a reason to delete, never a reason to delete QUIETLY.

Doctrine 18 · IN-STORY TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

"Technical Specifications" means THE IN-STORY SPECS, NEVER THE REAL ONES.

Given as a global standard with Mike's own forecast attached: "this drifts back easily."

WHAT A SPECIFICATION SURFACE IS. Any surface whose subject is what the machine is. Defined by SUBJECT, not by heading — a face that starts listing the machine's components becomes one the moment it does.

WHAT MAY NOT BE ON ONE: the board's part number, source-tree filenames, the module list, the bench sketch count, the count of lines of source, the I²C addresses, the pin numbers, the validation state of the workshop, and any calendar date of the real build. These are all TRUE. They are the provenance of a prop, and a spec sheet is not a provenance record.

WHAT IS NOT COVERED: the Record is the house's log of receiving the object THIS YEAR — a sealed modern bag, a USB-C adapter, a slow charge. All modern, all correct, on a surface whose subject is now. The rule is about which machine a spec sheet is describing, not about banning the present tense.

WHY IT DRIFTS BACK, WHICH IS THE PART WORTH KNOWING: the real facts are the ones Ops can VERIFY. A session looking for something true to put on a spec sheet reaches for the firmware tree every time, because the firmware tree is the thing it can read — and it will feel like diligence while it does it. Recording the law does not stop it; a gate does — npm run instory:gate.

AND IT BEARS ON TWO CONFLICTS IN THIS CATALOGUE, in a direction that is easy to miss: K-08 and K-09. "The in-story manual is the authority for the in-story spec, and the real firmware is not evidence about a 1965 machine at all." Register row N-i.

Doctrine 21 · EVERYTHING IN THE FORM IS STORY

Material arriving through the dictation instruments is in-story by construction, so Doctrine 11 is satisfied at the instrument and no future dictation gets re-adjudicated entry by entry.

IT DOES NOT EXEMPT OPS' OWN PROSE ANYWHERE, including inside a Record entry, and it does not lower the provenance bar.

Doctrine 22 · A LIMIT IS SHOWN WHERE THE STRING IS WRITTEN

He must never again discover a limit from a report.

A constraint has three halves: a render that cannot lie, a gate that refuses, and a live count on the field. WARN, NEVER BLOCK. Count what would be SAVED, not the raw field. Say where there is NO limit. The warning travels into the paste.

Doctrine 23 · THE RECORD'S REGISTER — EMAIL-LIKE, NOT AN EMAIL PROGRAM

No From, no To, no Subject line, no reply affordances, no inbox, no message headers, no envelope furniture of any kind. What is borrowed is the REGISTER ONLY: the plainness and the attachments-at-the-bottom convention.

THE TEST IS NOT is it useful — IT IS would a mail program have it.

Doctrine 24 · ONCE IT IS RULED GONE, IT IS GONE FROM HIS VIEW

Not archived where it resurfaces, not listed in a tracker, not carried in a register as a closed row he has to scroll past. A deleted thing is named ONCE, in the log of the round that killed it, and nowhere else.

THE TEST IS NOT is it archived, IT IS will he meet it again.

THIS CATALOGUE IS BOUND BY IT. Nothing on these pages is a resurrection of something Mike ruled gone. Where a struck thing is named — the credit reel, THE MORGUE, the _tmp/ line, MGK-65x — it is named once, in the row that records the striking, because a catalogue that does not record a strike invites the next session to rebuild it.

Doctrine 25 · THE TOOLS ARE FOR WORKING, NOT FOR BRIEFING

"All the stuff at the top, I never read."

Construction clause: on the field, in the footer, or on a reference page — never above the work. A LEGEND IS NOT A BRIEFING — the test is whether the page is unreadable without it.

Doctrine 26 · LEAD WITH WHAT HE MUST DO OR DECIDE

Every report to Mike opens with what is waiting on him. THE TEST IS NOT is it true AND NOT is it interesting — IT IS does this change what he does next? AN EMPTY ASK IS A COMPLETE REPORT.

THE PROVENANCE GATE

npm run provenance:gate runs beside lint and build on every packet. It fails if any visitor-facing string in src/ or index.html has no row in provenance/register.json, or any referenced image has no row in provenance/assets.json.

Classes: MIKE · VERIFIED · DERIVED · HOUSE · RESTATED.

APPROVAL IS NOT AUTHORSHIP. A sentence Mike approved that Ops wrote is filed RESTATED, not MIKE, and must never be re-marked — a paraphrase in his class is indistinguishable from his words a week later.

AND THE GATE CANNOT VERIFY THAT A DECLARATION IS TRUE. That is the largest hole in it and it is written down in provenance/README.md §4.
