What building them taught

  1. The Live Photo movies hold eleven frames each, a third of a second, not three. The demonstration flavor is those frames stretched into slow motion: the only motion in the set besides the turn. Real video of the hands does not exist yet.
  2. One nine-second clip carried half the portfolio. Every flavor that wants energy leans on the 4K turn, and there is one of it.
  3. The stills carry the slow flavors well. Catalogue and buttery want nothing the set lacks. The tray crop rule holds; the handled treatment works in motion.
  4. The hard-cut flavors starve without motion. The newsreel survives on rhythm; the gnashing is thin, the same turn six ways.
  5. The answer beat is a card. Everything before it exists; the glass plate is the one shot production cannot do without.
  6. The sound is bare: the adult, and the short in the glitch. A bed is the music-as-element question, not answered here.

The shot list this produces

Must have, for production at all

  • The glass plate: the Everyday's top screen, static, close, square on, lit, ten seconds. Tuesday's take 4, still owed. Nothing composites without it.
  • The four Everyday element takes as ruled 09-12: knob, turn, handheld, 4K on the tripod, sixty seconds each. Nine seconds proves the idea; it cannot carry a week.

Must have, for the Gambler's own reels

  • Real video of the hands with the kit, ten to twenty seconds a piece: chips stacked and pushed, coins poured, cards cut, dice thrown on the tray, the case opened from closed, the book opened to a page.
  • The case closed, top-down; the case in the hand.

Like to have, adds dimensions

  • Your backlog: the item on a lazy Susan, full revolutions in several orientations; the item on a stick from several angles; the camera on the lazy Susan panning past the unit.
  • The unit on the tray with the kit around it, one wide.
  • A second setting for the same takes.
  • Sound: the chips, the coins, the dice on the tray, recorded close, a library of the kit's own noises.
Best case, next best. The set is enough for the slow flavors and, treated, for the album's plates and papers. It is not enough for production reels: the answer beat has no glass and the motion is one clip. The must-have list is the gap, and it is short.
