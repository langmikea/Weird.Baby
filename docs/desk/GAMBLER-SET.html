What it serves

  • The album's kit and papers. Every object in the Gambler's kit row is here, plus two documents the catalogue does not list yet: the Garcia book and the Blue Book.
  • The cover. The case open, 0197 and 0198, is the full-artifact cover the 09-11 ruling asked for. A closed-case frame is not in the set.
  • The 4K clip is the first element take that exists, and the first colour-on-white material.
  • The slate card is the finished-state list under the rule of 09-12: the case's label, hold-downs, glued blocks and touch-up are still to do, and the faceplate is out of scope by your own ruling. So this set is the record of the kit before finishing, or a rehearsal of the setup. A fact about sequence, not a fault.
  • Not landed: Tuesday's Everyday element takes. The calendar row stays open; the 4K clip is a start on one of the four.
