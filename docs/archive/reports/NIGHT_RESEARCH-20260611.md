   paper greys. The color of the era the wallpaper is imitating.

### WB-COLOR — Easter egg VERIFIED, palette proposed (clean slate per Mike; gold not sacred)
**The story is real and documented** (Hagley Library/DuPont archive, Vintage Guitar, GuitarPlayer):
Fender's 1960s custom colors WERE DuPont automotive lacquers — Duco nitrocellulose and Lucite
acrylic, the same paint sprayed on Cadillacs and Chevys, offered on guitars at a 5% upcharge.
- **Sonic Blue = DuPont Duco 2295, a 1956 CADILLAC color** (Fender 1960–72). Beatles-associated,
  unpopular in its day → rare now; a '62 Sonic Blue Strat appraised at **$45,000** on Antiques
  Roadshow (2014).
- **Daphne Blue — borrowed from Cadillac. Surf Green — 1957 CHEVROLET (DuPont 2461).**
  Foam Green — Buick. Teal Green (Lucite 4297-L) — 1962 Lincoln "Riviera Turquoise."
- The loop-closer: Hunter's own bio claims "**60's psychedelic and surf rock flair**."
**Proposed palette (tuned for satin-paper UI, exact vintage codes don't map 1:1 to sRGB):**
- **W.B Ocean Turquoise `#2e6f6a`** — workhorse accent (legit Fender '65 chart name, GM-derived)
- **W.B Surf Green `#7fb2a3`** — secondary (THE surf-rock color, deepened for contrast)
- **W.B Sonic Blue `#9db8cc`** — reserved/whisper accent (the Cadillac/Beatles/$45K story)
**Backstory, one line: car paint → guitar paint → museum paint.** No pink, no yellow. ✅
Next step: live CSS-var mock so Mike judges on the actual site.
