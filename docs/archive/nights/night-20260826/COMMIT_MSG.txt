One surface: the Portal's controls on all four channels

CH3's surface is the target for every channel, so SCROLL, CLICK, POWER and
SHAKE follow the digit strip out of twin.html and the museum draws both rows
over television, the test signal, the photograph and the machine alike. It is
the same structural argument the strip left on in August rather than a second
one: a control living in that document can only ever appear on one channel of
four, because the overlay unmounts it. Framed() now removes #monctl exactly as
it already removed #monlayout, and the four handlers are untouched and still
the machine's only inputs -- the museum reaches them through a new contract
word, {wb:"portal-control"}, the mirror of the portal-close the twin has posted
back since S4. The power lamp travels the other way from Mon_Power_Sync's
existing 200ms chokepoint, and only on a change.

Two implementations on disk is forced rather than chosen: twin.html is
single-file and no-network by a standing constraint and must work with no
museum, so neither side can import the other. What they share is five numbers,
and every one was measured on the served page.

Scroll keeps its one meaning. Channels do not move to it, so the fault the
channel-selector scoping named -- one control, two meanings -- is not created,
and the ignoring on the three machineless channels is written down at the
listener that does it so a later round does not read a dead control as an
unfinished one.

Click tears as well as shuttering, and the scripted tear keeps its rarity
because the two run on separate clocks: a press advances nothing, resets
nothing and consumes no step. That the tear has no reason is recorded at the
tear as deliberate; an invented one would be worse than none. The slip moved
off the iframe onto a wrapper, because it had only ever reached one kind of
picture and the rip drew over television with nothing moving under it.

Channel 4 becomes an img. The plate always registered with the bezel; what was
wrong was the element, since object-fit does not apply to an iframe and the
browser's own image viewer was drawing it. The channel declares picture: true,
which is data in the same shape as bezel and note.

The selected channel is a hole rather than ink, which T4 and S2 both ordered in
July and neither reached this strip. Television's "TELEVISION ON THIS CHANNEL."
is struck with its register row, and the Portal track is renamed to "Launch the
Portal" -- recorded as a reversal and not as a correction, with canon carrying
six numbered steps so a later round can tell Ops fixing its own write-up from
Mike ruling differently.

The lint baseline moves to 9 errors and 7 warnings by a real removal; the first
cut of that edit silenced the warning with a disable instead, which moves the
number while changing nothing, and it was undone. monFeed has been pinned at 1
inside the museum since 2026-08-21 and is flagged rather than fixed as W-a. The
round log is at docs/MUSEUM_ONE_SURFACE_LOG-20260826.md and nothing was
deployed.
