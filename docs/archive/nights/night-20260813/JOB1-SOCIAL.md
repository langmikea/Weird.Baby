# 1a — THE PLATFORMS, AND THE ACCOUNT STRUCTURE

## THREE BRANDS, TWO POSTING ACCOUNTS, ONE SILENT ONE

**Weird.Baby posts nothing. It holds the name.**

Reasons, in order of weight:

1. **The house has no content of its own.** Everything it could post is better
   posted by Robots or by Music. A feed with nothing to say posts filler, and
   filler is what kills an account's reach before the real thing arrives.
2. **The one social promise on the site is in the ROBOTS FAQ**, not the house's
   (§1f). *"Follow us on social media"* is the answer to *"Can I buy one?"* — a
   question about the machines. The handle it needs is the Robots handle.
3. **You are one person for ninety days.** Every account you open is a thing
   that looks abandoned if you stop. Two can be sustained. Three cannot.

**The house handle still gets registered everywhere on Sunday** — as a
defensive hold and a redirect. An empty profile with a link to weird.baby is not
an abandoned account; it is a nameplate.

## ROBOTS AND MUSIC NEED SEPARATE ACCOUNTS — YES, PLAINLY

They are not two topics; they are two audiences with almost no overlap.

- **Robots** is a serialised fiction with a clock. Its reader wants the next
  instalment and will follow for the story. A song in that feed is an
  interruption.
- **Music** is your own work, with Worth A Listen under it. Its listener follows
  a musician. A fictional 1965 machine's maintenance log in that feed reads as
  the account being hacked.
- **Ninety days is the point.** A serialised thing lives or dies on its readers
  knowing what arrives and when. Mixing it with anything destroys that.

## THE RECOMMENDATION

| brand | posts on | holds the name on | posts nowhere else |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Weird.Baby** (house) | *nothing* | Instagram · Bluesky · TikTok · YouTube | everywhere |
| **Weird.Baby Robots** | **Instagram** · **Bluesky** | TikTok (activate week 3) | — |
| **Weird.Baby Music** | **YouTube** | Instagram · Bluesky | — |

### Why those and not the others

**Instagram — Robots' home.** The fiction is image-led: manual pages,
photographs of the machines, the Portal. Carousels are the native form for "here
are four pages of a document nobody has seen", and that is what this story is
made of. Nothing else does image-sequence as well.

**Bluesky — Robots' second, for one reason that outweighs its smaller
audience.** `@robots.weird.baby` is *free, unsquattable and self-evidently
authentic*. A serialised mystery whose account name is provably the same entity
as the website is worth more than reach. It also costs almost nothing to feed: a
still and a line.

**YouTube — Music's home, and it is already true.** The Worth A Listen wing is
built out of YouTube embeds; the artists you carry are YouTube channels; Hunter
Root's catalogue resolves to `youtube.com/@hunterrootmusic`. Music belongs where
the songs already play. **Do not start a music Instagram feed you have to
supply** — hold the name, post when there is a song.

**TikTok — reserved Sunday, activated in week three, and that is deliberate.**
The shorts bench (`npm run shorts`) exists to feed it and its four output shapes
already include 9:16. But a platform that rewards frequency is the wrong thing
to open on day one of a ninety-day run you are doing alone. **Get the Instagram
rhythm holding first.** Registering the handle Sunday costs five minutes and
protects the name.

### Noise — named so you can stop wondering

- **X / Twitter** — reach for a new account with no following is close to zero,
  and new signups are the most friction of any platform. It is a name to hold,
  not a channel. *Hold the handle only if it is free in under two minutes;
  otherwise skip it and do not come back to it.*
- **Facebook** — a Page needs a personal profile behind it and its organic reach
  for a new page is effectively nil. Skip entirely.
- **Threads** — comes free with the Instagram account and cross-posts with one
  toggle. **Do not treat it as a platform**; turn the cross-post on and forget
  it. Zero marginal effort, so it is not noise — but it is not a decision either.
- **Pinterest, LinkedIn, Tumblr, Snapchat** — wrong audience, wrong form, or
  dead for this. Skip.
- **Reddit** — the right audience for a robot ARG genuinely lives there, but you
  cannot broadcast to it; it requires participating as a person in communities
  with their own rules, and posting your own project reads as spam. **This is a
  real opportunity and a real trap, and it is not a Sunday task.** Revisit at
  week six with something to show.
- **Discord** — a server is a commitment to moderate, not a channel to post in.
  Not now. If the fiction gets an audience that wants to talk to each other,
  that is a good problem and you build it then.

# 1c — TIMING: THE NUMBER YOU CAN HOLD FOR NINETY DAYS ALONE

## Robots: THREE A WEEK. Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday.

**39 posts across the ninety days.** First post **Tuesday 18 August**, which is
Day 02 as ruled.

Why three and not daily:

- **The Record is daily; the feed is not, and conflating them is the failure
  mode.** You are already writing an entry every day. A social post is a
  *derivative* of that work, not a second job — but only if you are not obliged
  to make one every day.
- **Daily alone for ninety days does not survive contact with a bad week.** The
  first missed day of a daily account reads as abandonment. The first missed day
  of a three-a-week account reads as nothing at all.
- **Three a week is enough to stay in a feed.** Below two you are forgotten;
  above four you are making filler.

Fixed days matter more than the count. **Tue / Thu / Sat, and the same time each
day.** Pick the hour Sunday and never think about it again.

## Music: ONE A FORTNIGHT, and only when there is something.

Roughly **6 posts across the ninety days.** A music account with nothing to
release should be quiet. Quiet is not neglect; posting nothing is correct when
there is nothing.

## House: ZERO.

## The rule that makes it survivable

**Bank the buffer, do not post the buffer.** Any week you have five posts' worth
of material, post three and keep two. The account's job is to be *there* on
Thursday, not to be impressive on Tuesday.

**If you are going to miss a week — say nothing.** Do not post an apology. An
account that explains itself is an account that has started managing its
audience instead of showing them things.

## MUST BE DONE BEFORE SUNDAY — 10 minutes, do it tonight or Saturday

**Three email addresses.** Every Instagram account needs its own email address;
the same one will not serve two. YouTube needs a Google account. You own the

```
Cloudflare dashboard → weird.baby → Email → Email Routing
    robots@weird.baby   →  forwards to your real inbox
    music@weird.baby    →  forwards to your real inbox
    (papa@weird.baby already exists and is already on the site)
```

**Cloudflare Email Routing is free and it is in the same dashboard that serves
the site.** It needs MX records to propagate — usually minutes, occasionally an
hour. **This is the one thing that can lose you Sunday morning if you leave it
to Sunday morning.**

## WILL SLOW YOU DOWN ON THE DAY

**1. Instagram wants a phone number on every new account — and one number only
goes so far.** As of 2026 phone verification is mandatory at signup, and a
number that has been used a few times starts getting refused at the verification
step. You need two Instagram accounts (Robots, Music) plus the house hold.

**What to do:** create them **one at a time, from the same logged-in Instagram
via "Add account"** rather than as three cold signups, and give each its own
email. **If the second one refuses your number, stop and do it tomorrow** — do
not go looking for a virtual number, which is how accounts get flagged and
killed in week two. **Losing the handle to a delay is recoverable. Losing the
account to a flag is not.**

**2. DNS propagation on the Bluesky domain handles.** The TXT record is
instant to write and usually verifies in under five minutes, but it can take
longer. **Do all three TXT records first, then go do other platforms, then come
back and verify.** Do not sit and watch it.

**3. YouTube handle changes are rate-limited** — a small number of changes per
14 days. **Get it right the first time.** Decide the handle before you type it.

## CANNOT BE DONE SUNDAY AT ALL

- **Verification badges.** All of them need an established account and most need
  a following or a paid subscription. Not a Sunday item, not a month-one item.
- **Any follower count.** Obviously, but worth saying: Sunday produces empty
  accounts. That is what Sunday is for.
- **TikTok's real activation.** Handle only. Week three.
- **`twitter:site` on the share card** — see §1f; it needs a handle to exist
  first, so it is a five-minute repo change on Monday, not Sunday.
