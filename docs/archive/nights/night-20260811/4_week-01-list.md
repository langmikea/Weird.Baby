> **Criterion: it is what the story just did.**
>
> On the page: `3-1 INTRODUCTION` · `3-3 UNPACKING` · **Table 3-1 ITEMS TO BE

> itself and you do not have to write a word of connective tissue.**

> It is the densest page of the four — one table and six paragraphs — and it is
> worth it.

> #### 2. **page-24** — printed **5-2**, SECTION V · STARTING PROCEDURE
> **Criterion: it is what the story is about to do.**
>
> On the page: `Figure 5-1 STARTING SEQUENCE, SIMPLIFIED FLOW` · `5-3 FIRST
> INSTALLATION` · `5-5 FIRST RUN` · `5-7 ESTABLISHED OPERATION` · **`5-9

> sub-paragraphs on permitting it and postponing it — and week one is about

> **⚠ IT CARRIES `[ ART REQUIRED ]`.** Figure 5-1 is an empty box. Either you
> draw a simplified flow diagram, or this page ships with a visible hole. **This
> is the one recommendation with a blocker on it.**

> #### 3. **page-25** — printed **5-3**, SECTION V continued
