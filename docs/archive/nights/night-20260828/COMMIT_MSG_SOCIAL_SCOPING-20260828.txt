SCOPED AND UNBUILT, the shape docs/HIDDEN_LINKS_SCOPING-20260807.md already
uses in this directory. Nothing in this commit is a mechanism; it is the
shape a build would take, written before the build so the shape can be
argued rather than discovered.

MIKE RULED FOUR THINGS AND THEY DECIDE THE ARCHITECTURE BETWEEN THEM. The
museum does not assemble reels, he makes them, so there is no encoder in
this. The museum runs daily operations but YOUTUBE'S OWN SCHEDULER
publishes, so nothing here calls an API, holds a token or fires at nine in
the morning. Facebook is a channel with no scheduler, captured when he
posts it, because its scheduling moves every year and lives behind a
Business Suite that is not stable to build against, and one platform
working beats two half-wired. What those four force is that the museum
holds the intention and the record while the platform holds the mechanism,
and every hard problem left is therefore a file problem.

A POST IS ONE OBJECT AND NOT TWO. It starts as an intention and becomes a
receipt, which is the Record's own property and the most valuable thing in
the building to copy: there is no second file recording that Record 003
went out, because the entry's existence IS that record. Four states rather
than three, and the fourth is forced by the ruling: staged means uploaded
and scheduled at YouTube and invisible to the museum, and without it
nothing can tell "not made" from "made and waiting", which is the whole
difference between a gap and a false alarm.

A SCHEDULE IS A RULE THAT GENERATES DUE DATES AND NOT A LIST SOMEBODY
FILLS. The argument is not preference and the proof is already in the
tree: SURFACING_LOG.md is a good instrument meant to run at every session
close and it stopped on 2026-08-06, twenty-two days and sixteen rounds
ago. But the rule generates a QUESTION rather than an obligation, because
a gap in the numbers is not a defect and a later round must not fix one.
The house's silence is a DECLARED schedule carrying its reason, never an
absence, or a later round finds a wing without one and helpfully gives it
one.

PERFORMANCE IS A DATED READING HE PASTES, APPEND-ONLY, and stamped with
the day he READ it rather than the day he typed it, which is the hazard
this tree has already filed twice about tools that stamp saved at the
moment of saving. A count overwritten in place destroys the trend and the
trend is the entire point.

WHAT MUST NOT BE BUILT TWICE IS NAMED ROW BY ROW: the shelf, because a
second reader of it would be a parallel list on the day it was written;
record-shape.mjs's budgets-and-constraints pattern with its silent:true
column; the day editor, because a separate post editor would be the SED
test failing on its own first instance; reveal/day.mjs's report shape;
and surfacing.mjs, which already asks what a planner would ask and should
be restarted rather than reimplemented. A post is NOT a Record entry and
must not become one, and the one real duplication risk is the calendar:
the schedules take their own anchors and must not read RECORD_EPOCH, or
moving the epoch silently reschedules a quarter of social.
