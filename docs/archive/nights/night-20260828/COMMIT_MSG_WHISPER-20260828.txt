═══ THE HANDLES, AND OPS WAS WRONG ABOUT THE DOT ═══════════════════════

The TikTok account exists: @papaweird.baby, display name Weird.Baby, bio
empty. Ops told Mike that TikTok does not permit dots in handles and that
is FALSE — the handle has one. The claim was asserted from memory as a
platform fact, nothing checked it, and it reached him as a constraint with
a recommendation built on top of it, that the dot must move out of the
handle into the display name. He did not need that advice and it was
wrong. A platform's rules are not in this repository and Ops cannot read
them from memory; if a platform constraint is load-bearing it is observed
on the platform or carried as UNVERIFIED and named as such. The claim is
kept as HANDLE.retractedClaim, marked false rather than deleted, because a
struck claim that is removed reads to a later round as a claim nobody ever
made and this one reached Mike.

What is observed is all that is recorded: TikTok @papaweird.baby, YouTube
@PapaWeirdBaby, Instagram unset with the account itself unstated, Facebook
unset with the account existing. The two known handles are close but NOT
identical and are recorded as two real handles rather than reconciled into
one, and neither unset handle is inferred from them.

═══ THE GUESTBOOK — SURVEYED, AND NOTHING WAS TOUCHED ══════════════════

Ops ruled nothing is deleted this round and nothing was: no wrangler
command was run, read or write. The survey answers what was asked. There
are two backup tools and both are read-only by their own header. There is
no tool that writes or deletes, and no DELETE route anywhere in the

AND THE BACKUP ANSWER IS THE FINDING. The newest guestbook-only dump is
2026-06-03 and the newest full-DB export that contains the table is
2026-07-01, holding three rows. The live site shows seven signatures. Four
live signatures exist in no backup at all — Sammy B, Mo, Tommy, and the
2026-08-17 entry Mike wants removed. The scheduled job has failed twice,
on 2026-07-01 where an npm notice on stdout was captured as an error and
on 2026-08-01 at the Cloudflare API, and the only record of either is a
log nobody reads. So the tree has a backup mechanism that worked, stopped
on 2026-07-01, and has been failing silently since.

The one act that should happen regardless of how he rules is running
tools/backup-guestbook.ps1 once, because it is read-only and it captures
