AND OPS WAS WRONG ABOUT THE DOT, WHICH IS THE HALF OF THIS COMMIT WORTH
READING. Ops told Mike that TikTok does not permit dots in handles. That is
false; @papaweird.baby has one. The claim was asserted from memory as a
platform fact and nothing checked it — not measured, not looked up, not
carried as unverified where it mattered. It reached Mike as a constraint
and a recommendation was built on top of it, that the dot must move out of
the handle and into the display name. He did not need that advice and it
was wrong.

THE LESSON IS THE GENERAL ONE AND IT IS RECORDED IN BOTH FILES. A
platform's rules are not in this repository and Ops cannot read them from
memory. They change, they differ per surface, and they are exactly the
class of fact this project already refuses to invent — a handle is not
something Ops may invent, and neither is the rule about what a handle may
contain. If a platform constraint is load-bearing it is observed on the
platform, or it is carried as UNVERIFIED and named as such. The claim is
kept as HANDLE.retractedClaim and marked false rather than deleted, because
a struck claim that is removed reads to a later round as a claim nobody
ever made, and this one reached Mike.

WHAT IS OBSERVED IS ALL THAT IS RECORDED. TikTok @papaweird.baby, YouTube
@PapaWeirdBaby, Instagram unset with the account itself unstated, Facebook
unset with the account existing. The two known handles are close but NOT
identical — different casing, and only one carries the dot — and they are
recorded as two real handles rather than reconciled into one. Neither unset
handle is inferred from them, because a pattern across two surfaces is not
a fact about a third, and M60 stays open for those two. Note also that both
handles he actually made carry Papa, which appears in none of his three
stated preferences, so the preference list is a record of what he asked for
and not a rule the observed handles break.
