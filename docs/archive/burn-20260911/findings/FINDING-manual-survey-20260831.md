## 0 · WHAT THIS FILE IS NOT, AND THE READER SHOULD KNOW FIRST

**This file was asked to carry a survey produced in a packet called B, and that
packet did not run in the session that is writing this.** Four of the five
things it was asked for are measurable from the tree and are measured here. One
is not:

> **THE SIXTEEN SEED CLUSTERS, RANKED BY POSITIONS UNBLOCKED, ARE NOT IN THIS
> FILE.** That is an analysis — which unwritten positions share a single missing
> decision, and how many each decision would release — and it was produced
> somewhere this session cannot read. **It is not reconstructed here, because a
> reconstruction that came out at fifteen clusters or seventeen would be
> indistinguishable from the original and would quietly replace it.** If that
> survey exists in another window it should be pasted in under this heading. If
> it does not, §5 below is the raw material it would be built from.

**And the headline number in the instruction is out of date, which is worth
recording rather than quietly correcting.** The packet says 102 remaining
positions. **Measured today: 95.** The difference reconciles exactly — Section
IX's six positions and Appendix F's F-1 were written after that count was
taken. `102 − 6 − 1 = 95`. Nothing is wrong with the 102; it was true when it
was measured, and it is the third number this week to go stale by being
correct at the time.

## 3 · THE ROADBLOCKS — what stops a position being written at all

**These are not conflicts to be ruled between two readings. They are absences.**

- **D-5 / D-6 — the specification cannot be completed.** Mass and dimensions
  have never been taken. `robots:tools/manual_build.py:130` has said so since
  2026-08-02: *"These are measurements, not decisions, and they belong to the
  bench."* **Table 2-1 without a mass and a size does not read as a
  specification**, and Section II is eleven unwritten positions built around it.
- **D-11 — replaceable parts.** Nothing exists and nothing is planned. **Every
  model of the period ends with a parts list; this one ends with an empty
  table.** Table 11-1 and most of Section XI wait on it.
- **SCAT is a name with nothing behind it.** Position 10-11 exists because the
  name does. Nobody has said what SCAT is or how it differs from BIST.
- **AMMMS, PHVDC, MIALLO, MindsEye, MAME** — named once each, never expanded.
  **Appendix G's expansions are the position that cannot be written**, and two
  of its rows already print `[ EXPANSION REQUIRED ]` on the page.
- **The television is in no file.** The corpus has a bezel, an opening, a feed,
  channels and a source dial, and **no receiver** — nothing names the glass the
  feed lands on, its size, its type, its warm-up or its catalogue number.
- **Appendix F's 35 meanings.** The terms are real and every meaning is empty.
  This is one position by count and thirty-five decisions by content.

## 6 · THE THING THAT OUTRANKS ALL OF THE ABOVE

**Every one of the 55 written positions is in a register Mike ruled wrong on
2026-08-31.** He read paragraph 9-1 and could not follow it. The Retro
Encabulator model is retired; the manual is plain instruction prose. 9-1 is
rewritten and is the only paragraph in the document in the new voice.

**So the census in §1 overstates what is done.** Fifty-four positions are
written in the voice he could not read, and a register pass over Sections IV,
V, VI, VIII and Appendix B comes before any new position is written. **A
survey of what is left to write, read on its own, would send the next session
to Section I.** That would be the wrong move and this paragraph exists to stop
it.
