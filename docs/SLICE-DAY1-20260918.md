## What it means

The reel line and the queue are sound: a stand-in went from the ledger to a
dated slot in under two minutes with no hands. **The walk breaks the moment
the stranger leaves the reel.** Steps 3 to 8 are one gap seen five times:
there is no room for a reel to land in. The board already carries the pieces
(`landing`, `no-dead-ends`, `album-everyman`); the slice says they are one
piece of work with one shape, and that shape is not drawn yet:

> one address per day's drop, that opens the album on the day's track with
> the machine in it, already awake, showing only what is released.

Everything after it (the daily drop, every reel's caption, the ask reel, the
shoot reels) inherits that one shape. Same except data.

## Corrections to the plan

- The Q&A reel's build is 2 minutes, not a session. Fifteen is half an hour of machine time.
