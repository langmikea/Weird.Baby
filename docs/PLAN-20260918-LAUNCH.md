## 2. How the work is done (the rules of v2)

1. **Range first.** Where we do not know what we want, the first gate is a
   range: name the limits, make a quick rough example of each, Mike points.
   Open ranges today: the beat under the reels (a palette he can play), the
   run of show, the shoot reel's look, the ask reel's variants, the song's
   page, the wing's shapes (the five mocks, in hand).
2. **Pilot before volume.** One through the whole pipe on **stand-ins**
   (footage in hand, the tray and 09-16 sets, generated images) before
   anything is asked in volume; and nothing is asked in volume until one
   real post has gone through the queue.
3. **Mike's material at the last responsible moment.** His gates carry a
   window (can start, needed by), not a date. His calendar rows move only by
   his word.
4. **A stand-in never ships.** The provenance gate refuses an undeclared
   string; the rehearsal (10-22) is the check that no stand-in image, word or
   sound is left in the launch build.
5. **No date is typed.** Durations, predecessors, fixed events and the
   launch walls go in; a forward and a backward pass give every gate its
   forecast, its needed-by and its float. When a pilot teaches a new
   duration, one number changes and every date re-derives.

What this does to Mike's lane: the fourteen dated volume rows become three
weeks of pointing at ranges, one fifteen-minute shoot pilot, one real Q&A,
ten minutes of Buffer clicks; the shoots, the words and the fifteen Q&As
follow their pilots, inside windows that run to mid and late October
(`docs/desk/BOARD-SCHEDULE.md`, "What is asked of Mike").

## 3. 50,000 ft: is it purpose built, or an assembly of tasks?

v1 was an assembly: its columns came from the description of the board, its
rows from the task list and the catalogue, its dates from the 09-10 weekly
themes. Tested against the walk above, four things do not hold:

1. **Half the board serves no stranger.** 24 of 54 things carry "proven on
   the real unit", and the real unit's chain is the riskiest in the plan
   (firmware never run on hardware, zero float). Nobody on the walk touches
   the real unit: there is no way to buy at the door, and the glass in every
   reel is the twin's by ruling (09-12). Proposed: the bench stays as one
   thing, for its own reasons; "proven on the real unit" leaves the 24
   ladders and goes to later. Not applied; his 09-10 words asked for the
   unit, so it is his to rule.
2. **Two reel lines have no slot.** The ask reel and the shoot reel are
   being built with no day to post on and no room to land in. What every
   line exists to fill is a **run of show** (what posts each day from the door on,
   and where it lands), and the plan had none. Added, range first.
3. **The walk ends at step 4.** /R's purpose is robot sales, and at the
   door a stranger who wants one can do nothing, not even say so. The shop
   is ruled out until after the door (2A), rightly; a line and a "tell me
   when" is not the shop. This is the one strategic gap, and it is a
   question for Mike, not a build.
4. **The launch-run pages are all walled at the door.** A feature that is
   promoted on day 12 of the run needs its page by day 12, not by 10-25.
   Walling the product rows by their own day would take a third of Ops'
   October build off the critical weeks. Proposed, not applied (the day
   numbers need checking against the run of show first).

What does hold: the SED strategy (five of seven lines take almost nothing
from Mike per unit), the two launches, the outcomes, and the board.

## 7. How the plan is kept (as much as needed, and not more)

- **One source.** `BOARD-PLAN.json`. The board, the grade and the schedule
  are generated. Nothing is kept in two places.
- **Status at any point.** `npm run board` today; `--as-of DATE` for any
  day, which is also how a change is tested before it is ruled. The 06:08
  run grades every morning; one line in MORNING.md.
- **Who updates what.** Mike updates nothing. Ops marks a gate earned in
  the session the evidence lands, with the evidence named.
- **Cadence.** Daily: the grade. Sunday: the sitting is two things only:
  **point** (the ranges that are ready) and **rule the reds** (3C: cut,
  move, push, accept), each logged in `docs/desk/BOARD-DECISIONS.md`.
- **Change control.** Baseline v2. After the must-have list is ruled, a
  wall, a duration, a predecessor or a must-have entry changes only by a
  logged ruling. Tasks are not the baseline.
- **What the exec sees.** One page, the board: red only; how big, where, who.
