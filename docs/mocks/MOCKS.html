3.3How you work itscroll knob · click igniter · shake

  1. Turn the scroll knob to PROBABILITIES and click the igniter.
  2. Choose: a coin, a number, a card, dice, or a lottery line.
  3. Shake. The glass answers. The answer is final until you shake again.
