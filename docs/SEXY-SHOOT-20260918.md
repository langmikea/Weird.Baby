## 2. The rig (one setup, every session)

- **The ground:** a white sweep. A roll or two sheets of white poster board,
  taped to the table and curved up the wall behind, so there is no horizon
  line. Big enough that the case, open, sits on it with a hand's width around.
- **The light:** soft, from two sides. A north window and a white card
  opposite it, or two lamps through a white sheet at about 45 degrees left
  and right. No flash, no overhead room light, no mixed colour (all daylight
  or all lamp).
- **The camera:** the iPhone 15, main lens (1x; 2x for a small thing; never
  pinch past 2x), on the tripod. 24 MP, Most Compatible. Press and hold to
  lock focus and exposure on the item, then drag the sun down until the white
  behind it is bright but the item's highlights still hold.
- **Video:** 4K at 60, HDR Video off (Settings › Camera › Record Video),
  held upright (9:16), the DJI pair off; the room's sound is not used.
- **Hands:** the white gloves, whenever a hand is in frame. A lint brush and
  a blower before every item.
- **No rule, no scale, nothing else on the white.**

The first three frames of 09-27 (a hero, a detail, a document) are the
proof. Ops processes them the same day; if the rig needs a change, only
those three are re-shot.

## 3. The grammar: the same frames for every item

| code | frame | for |
|---|---|---|
| S0 | the slate: the card with the item's name, on the white | the first frame of every item |
| S1 | the hero: three-quarter, eye level, the whole item | every item; the unit's S1 is the album cover |
| S2 | square on, front | units, cases, boxes, books |
| S3 | the back | units, cases, boxes, books |
| S4 | top-down, clean | everything flat or small: chips, coins, cards, tools, papers |
| S5 | the underside | units only |
| S6 | open, and the contents laid out | anything that opens |
| S7 | two or three details: a plate, a glass, a latch, a worn corner | units and cases |
| S8 | the group: the whole kit together, the "for sale" frame | once per album |
| V1 | the turn: ten seconds, the unit turned slowly in gloved hands or on a turntable, one full turn | once per unit |
| V2 | the unboxing: one continuous take from 45 degrees, gloved hands open the box or case and lay the kit out on the white; thirty to sixty seconds; no talking | once per album |
| V3 | three close passes of five seconds: the camera drifts past the plate, the glass, the best detail | once per unit |

A document (an ad, a page, a spread) is S4 only: flat, top-down, the whole
sheet with white around it, no hands; a book adds S1, S3 and the spine.

## 5. The drop, and what Ops does with it

Originals go where the desk already looks: OneDrive › WeirdBaby › photos ›
everyman (gambler, ceo, informer). Tell Code "photos in". Within two days:
manifest rows from the slates (`photos/manifest.json`, shoot dated, treatment
"white"), the prints built, the album's cover, plates, kit and papers
replaced, the clips filed for the reel line.

Ops owes before 09-27: the "white" treatment in `tools/photos-build.py`
(find the subject, clean the ground to white, square the crop, no border, no
grain), proven on the three proof frames. Ops owes after the first footage:
the story for the reel shape the clips feed (the turn, the unboxing), written
before anything is cut.
