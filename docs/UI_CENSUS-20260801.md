# THE UI CENSUS — 2026-08-01

**What this is:** the honest map of the museum's UI *as it stands today*, route
by route, measured rather than remembered. Every figure below came out of a
headless browser walking the built site (`dist/client`) and reading the live
DOM — element rects, computed styles, real `scrollHeight` vs `clientHeight`.
Nothing here is from source-reading or recollection.

**Measurement conditions.** Chrome headless, built output served over
`127.0.0.1`, animations and transitions disabled, opacity fade-ins forced to 1
so first paint is the settled layout. Two reference viewports:
**1582×904** (a 1600×1000 window) and **504×804** (a 430×900 window). Where a
number depends on a stored user preference, the preference is stated.

> **A caution about "visible".** The census counts an element as visible when
> it renders with non-zero area and is not `display:none` / `visibility:hidden`.
> It does **not** know whether something is behind a fixed bar or scrolled out
> of view. Where that distinction matters it is called out explicitly.

## 1. THE SHARED SPINE — what /hr, /wb and /robots have in common

Three routes are the **same component** (`Exhibit.jsx`) driven by different
artist configs. This is the single most important fact in the census: their
differences are *data*, not code.

| region | class | role |
|---|---|---|
| title bar | `.ex-nav` | **fixed**, `z=90`, 45px tall at 1582 wide |
| album deck | `.cf-wrap` | the carousel, height user-resizable |
| deck handle | `.cf-dh` | row-resize drag, 14px tall |
| album banner | `.ex-album-banner` | the album title, 49px |
| the viewer | `.ex-main` | tracklist + panel, split left/right |
| split handle | `.vr-dh` | col-resize drag, 10px wide, full height of the viewer |
| body handle | `.bd-dh` | row-resize, **/robots only** |
| player bar | `.pb` | **fixed**, `z=100`, 68px tall, pinned to the viewport floor |

**Measured at 1582×904:**

| | /hr | /wb | /robots |
|---|---|---|---|
| page height | **6,585px** | 941px | 1,481px |
| `.ex-main` height | 591px | 436px | **880px** |
| deck `.cf-wrap` | 300px | 300px | 300px |
| viewer bottom (doc y) | — | — | 1,297 |
| `.bd-dh` present | no | no | **yes** |
| visible iframes | **17** | 1 | 1 |
| visible buttons | 22 *(127 in DOM)* | 9 | 9 |
| `cursor:pointer` elements | **277** | 72 | 42 |
| localStorage keys | `wb-hr-deck-width`, `wb-hr-presets` | *(none)* | *(none at rest)* |

**The three viewer heights come from three different mechanisms**, and this is
worth stating plainly because it looks like a bug and is not:

- **/wb — 436px.** No `bodyKey`; the viewer is `flex:1` and takes what the
  page has left.
- **/hr — 591px.** Same, but /hr has content *below* the exhibit
  (`section.hr-section`, 420px, at doc y 1008 and repeating), so the flex
  child settles differently.
- **/robots — 880px.** The only route declaring `bodyKey`, so the viewer has a
  persisted, user-dragged height (`wb-rb-bodyh`), defaulting to 880.

**/hr is a different SHAPE of page, not just a different album.** It is the
exhibit *plus* a stack of `section.hr-section` blocks — which is why its page
is 6,585px, why it has 17 YouTube iframes live at once, and why it is the only
route whose snap targets tile (see §5).

## 2. ROUTE BY ROUTE

### `/` — the home split

- `div.wb-root` → `div.wb-left` (791×904) + `div.wb-right` (791×904), plus a
  90×20 footer at y866.
- **Page height 904 — exactly the viewport. Nothing scrolls.**
- No fixed elements, no resize handles, no scroll containers.
- 5 buttons, 1 input, 1 textarea, 13 `cursor:pointer` elements.
- Touches no storage.

### `/hr` — the exhibit plus sections

- The full shared spine, then repeating `section.hr-section` blocks.
- **Page height 6,585px** — by far the longest route.
- Two fixed bars: `.ex-nav` (z90) and `.pb` (z100).
- Five resize-handle nodes reported, which is **two real handles**: `.cf-dh`
  (deck height) and `.vr-dh` (the split). The other three are `.cf-dh-line`
  ×2 and `.cf-dh-dot` — decorative children that inherit the cursor.
- **17 visible iframes.** Every YouTube embed on the page is live at once.
- **127 buttons in the DOM, 22 visible** — the rest are inside collapsed or
  off-screen sections.
- Persists `wb-hr-deck-width` and `wb-hr-presets`.

### `/wb` — the exhibit, minimal

- The shared spine and nothing after it. Page height 941px, 37px of scroll.
- Viewer 436px. 6 `select` elements, 1 iframe.
- Persists nothing.

### `/robots` — the exhibit, with the extra handle

- The shared spine **plus `.bd-dh`** at doc y 1297 — the body-height handle,
  the only one of its kind in the museum.
- Page height 1,481px; viewer 880px.
- 9 buttons, 1 iframe (the twin only appears when the portal is opened —
  **0 twins at rest**, verified in the control-room round).
- Persists `wb-rb-bodyh`, `wb-rb-split`, `wb-rb-cfh` once the visitor drags
  anything; nothing at first visit.

### `/shop` — the flat page

- `div.gift-shop` (960px wide, centred) with a fixed `.gift-shop__nav` and two
  298px sections.
- Page height 932px, 28px of scroll. **No resize handles, no scroll
  containers, 4 links, no buttons.**
- The simplest route in the museum by an order of magnitude.

### `/hr/media`, `/booth`

- `/hr/media` renders an 82px div and nothing else at this viewport — it is a
  stub or a redirect target, not a page. **Flagged, not diagnosed.**

## 3. RESIZE BEHAVIOUR — the three drags

| handle | axis | range | persisted as | routes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `.cf-dh` | deck height | `CF_MIN 160` – `CF_MAX 440` | `cfKey` | all three |
| `.vr-dh` | split % | `SPLIT_MIN 4` – `SPLIT_MAX 92` | `splitKey` | all three |
| `.bd-dh` | viewer height | `BODY_MIN 260` – `BODY_MAX 1600` | `bodyKey` | **/robots only** |

**All three are pointer-driven with `touch-action:auto`** — which means on a
touch device the browser may claim the gesture as a page scroll before the
handle sees it. Not tested on hardware; flagged as a real risk, not a finding.

**The split is `minmax(0, Nfr)` on a CSS grid.** Before the guest's-table
round it was plain `Nfr`, which floored the tracklist at its min-content width
(~24%) regardless of the constant — a fixed default that could never be
reached. Now measured: drag hard left → **4.0%**, hard right → **91.4%**.

**The viewer height default may only GROW to fit, never shrink.** A
fit-to-viewport effect used to pull the default down to whatever the viewport
had spare, which silently cancelled the generous default.

## 4. SCROLL BEHAVIOUR — and where the guest gets trapped

**At 1582×904 with the viewer at its 880px default, nothing on any route
overflows.** That is why the trap is easy to miss from a desk.

`.vp-face-body` (`overflow-y:auto`) is the panel that holds a track's content.
Measured, MGK-VIIIp, per track:

| track | 1582×904, viewer 880 | 504×804, viewer 880 |
|---|---|---|
| The Record | body 876 / content 1058 → **182px hidden** | body 310 / content 843 → **533px hidden** |
| The Manual | body 876 / content 1022 → **146px hidden** | body 310 / content 872 → **562px hidden** |
| The Firmware | fits | body 310 / content 599 → **289px hidden** |
| The Portal | fits | body 310 / content 448 → **138px hidden** |
| FAQ | fits | body 310 / content 580 → **270px hidden** |

**On a phone, every single track is trapped.** The panel's usable height
collapses to 310px because the columns stack, while the content does not
shorten — so between 138px and 562px of every track sits below an inside-panel
scroll boundary.

**And the front desk traps at laptop size too.** At 1262×624 with the viewer
at 460: Welcome 66px hidden, FAQ **227px**, Contact 18px.

**THE SHAPE OF THE TRAP, stated once.** The panel scrolls *inside itself*. When
its content ends, the page behind it still has scroll left — so the guest
reaches the bottom of what they can see, the page looks finished, and the
wheel does nothing they expect. The page-level scroll and the panel-level
scroll are two scrolls in the same gesture space with no visible boundary
between them.

**The fixed player bar makes it worse at the bottom edge.** `.pb` is 68px tall
and pinned to the viewport floor on every exhibit route — it covers doc-space
that the panel believes it owns. At 904 tall it occupies y 836–904; at 624 it
occupies y 556–624, **11% of the screen**.

## 5. SNAP BEHAVIOUR

`html { scroll-snap-type: y proximity; scroll-behavior: smooth }` is declared
globally.

- **/hr has targets that TILE** — its `section.hr-section` blocks are each
  roughly a screen tall, so wherever a reader stops there is a target nearby
  and snapping only tidies.
- **The exhibit carries no targets.** Two were added and then removed after
  they made the album deck unreachable: with targets only at the banner and
  the viewer, every scroll position from 80 to 320 was pulled to 360, leaving
  **four rest positions on the whole page** and the deck visible from only one
  of them. Measured before removal: 8 of 11 sampled positions yanked, worst
  yank 280px. After removal: **11 rest positions, 0 yanked.**

**The rule that came out of it:** snap targets must tile the region a guest
browses, or they must not exist. Two targets at one end of a long page is the
worst of both.

## 6. WHERE THE ROUTES GENUINELY DIVERGE

| behaviour | /hr | /wb | /robots | /shop | / |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| exhibit engine | yes | yes | yes | no | no |
| viewer height draggable | no | no | **yes** | — | — |
| snap targets | **yes (tiling)** | no | no | no | no |
| live iframes at rest | **17** | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| track faces | none | none | **all 5** | — | — |
| content below the exhibit | **yes** | no | no | — | — |
| persists at first visit | **yes** | no | no | no | no |

**`face` is a /robots-only contract.** `hunter-root-spine.js` declares **zero**
faces; /hr and /wb tracks are video lists. Everything the face machinery draws
— the register, the entries, the instrument panel, the still plate — exists on
one route only. This matters for any migration estimate: **changing the face
renderer cannot affect /hr or /wb.**

## 8. WHAT THE CENSUS DID NOT COVER

Stated so the map's edges are honest:

- **No touch-hardware testing.** All pointer behaviour is synthetic. The
  `touch-action:auto` risk on the three drag handles is a reading of the CSS,
  not an observation.
- **`/admin` and `/p/:id`** were not walked.
- **`/hr/media` returned an 82px stub** and was not diagnosed.
- **No keyboard-navigation or screen-reader pass.** Tab order, focus rings and
  landmark structure are unmeasured.
- **The 105 invisible /hr buttons** were counted, not explained.
- **Only two viewports.** Everything between 504 and 1582 wide is interpolated,
  not measured.
