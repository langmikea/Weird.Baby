## What building them taught

1. **The Live Photo movies hold eleven frames each**, a third of a second,
   not three seconds. The demonstration flavor is those eleven frames
   stretched by motion interpolation. It reads as slow motion of the hands
   and it is the only motion in the set apart from the turn. Real video of
   the hands does not exist yet.
2. **One nine-second clip carried half the portfolio.** The 4K turn is in
   the glitch, the gnashing and could be in any of the others. Every flavor
   that wants energy leans on it, and there is only one of it.
3. **The stills carry the slow flavors well.** The catalogue and the buttery
   drift want nothing the set does not have. The tray crop rule holds: the
   brown edge is out of every frame. The handled treatment works in motion.
4. **The hard-cut flavors starve without motion.** The newsreel survives on
   rhythm; the gnashing is thin, the same turn six ways.
5. **The answer beat is a card.** Everything before it exists; the glass
   plate is the one shot production cannot do without.
6. **The sound is bare**: the adult and, in the glitch, the short. A bed
   under the middle is the music-as-element question, not answered here.

## The shot list this produces

**Must have, for production at all**
- The glass plate: the Everyday's top screen, static, close, square on, lit,
  ten seconds (Tuesday's take 4, still owed). Nothing composites without it.
- The four Everyday element takes as ruled 09-12: knob, turn, handheld, in
  4K on the tripod, sixty seconds each. The nine-second turn proves the idea;
  it cannot carry a week.

**Must have, for the Gambler's own reels**
- Real video of the hands with the kit, ten to twenty seconds a piece: the
  chips stacked and pushed, the coins poured, the cards cut, the dice thrown
  on the tray, the case opened from closed, the book opened to a page.
- The case closed, top-down, and the case in the hand.

**Like to have, adds dimensions**
- Mike's backlog of 09-16: the item on a lazy Susan, full revolutions in
  several orientations; the item on a stick from several angles; the camera
  on the lazy Susan panning past the unit.
- The unit on the tray with the kit around it, one wide.
- A second setting for the same takes, as he planned.
- Sound: the chips, the coins, the dice on the tray, recorded close, as a
  library of the kit's own noises.

## Best case, next best

Mike's frame of 09-16: best case the set is enough to auto-produce the reels;
next best we agree the extra shots. Ops' read: the set is enough for the slow
flavors and for the album's plates and papers after treatment, and it is not
enough for production reels, because the answer beat has no glass and the
motion is one clip. The must-have list above is the gap, and it is short.
