The Friday formula

THE FRIDAY FORMULA your rule · Ops wording

It is a form, not a one-off. Fridays are built this way.

  • The entry summarises the day the way any other day's entry would.
  • Then four o'clock happens INSIDE it — not after it, not next week.
  • It is never “to be continued.” That is only making people wait.
  • The reader got everything they came for, and then the floor moves.

Both weeks end on it: a password short enough, then a box on a porch at four o'clock. Two Fridays is the first evidence the form holds — see X‑4.

How the worksheet keeps what you type

In the browser, on your machine, and nowhere else. Your responses live in this browser's local storage under one key, so a reload — or closing the tab and coming back — finds them where you left them. They are never written into the repository, which is also why npm run dictation can regenerate the left-hand column without touching a word of yours.

The consequence, stated because it is the one that could cost you an hour: a different browser, a different machine, or a cleared cache is a different worksheet. Press copy everything and paste it somewhere before you switch. If the browser refuses storage at all, the worksheet says so in red at the top rather than letting the typing evaporate quietly.
