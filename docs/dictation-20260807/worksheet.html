The calendar date

It has an exact format and nothing complains when it is wrong. four digits, a hyphen, two digits, a hyphen, two digits — 2026-08-10 — anything else parses to nothing at all, and the entry still renders: it just quietly has no dateline, no week number and no month band. That silence is why this box checks the shape while you type.
