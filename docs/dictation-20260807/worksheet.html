The weekly headlines are not on this page any more. They are all twelve together on the twelve-week table — asking for week one’s headline here and again there would be one question with two answers, in two browser stores, neither able to see the other. This page is the ten days.

Everything that explains how any of this works — the rails, the transfer classes, the standing rules, the checks against the tree, the three trackers — is on the reference page, and none of it is on this one.
