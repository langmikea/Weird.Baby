The story outline · week one, days 1–5

the cue card made real — and the honest half of it is how much of the card is blank

THE FINDING FIRST, BECAUSE IT CHANGES WHAT THIS PAGE COULD BE. The brief says week 1 as it stands — the week's headline, each day's headline, each day's topics with their weights. None of that exists. Both repositories were searched for a day-by-day story outline, a week headline, a topic list and a weighting: there is no such document, no such data file, and no such field. What exists is the transfer arc (which is weekly and about how material ARRIVED, not about what a day is about), the three-act story arc (which is about the whole product and names no days), and one Record entry.

SO THE FRAME IS BUILT AND THE CONTENT IS NOT. Doctrine 12 puts the content of the story with you, and writing five plausible day headlines would have been the exact failure mode the brief guards against — you would have read them back tomorrow and had no way to tell which of them you had said.

HOW TO READ THE COLOURS, AND IT IS THE ONLY RULE ON THE PAGE: a gold rail means the material is YOURS — today every gold rail is empty. A blue rail means Ops derived it from a rule, and the rule is named. Nothing is unlabelled.
