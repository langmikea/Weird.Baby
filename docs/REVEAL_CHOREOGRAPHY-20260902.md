# THE REVEAL CHOREOGRAPHY — third cut, 2026-09-02

**A proposal for Mike, not a landing.** Nothing is written into the reveal ledger's `when` column until he says so. It places every asset on hand across weeks two to twelve, one Record a weekday at 17:00, under his rules of 2026-09-02 (all in `docs/ARC.md` §6): *the story explains the device; every feature can be a day; things come out in phases; how it works is never itself a day; one character a week; a day's feature excludes nothing else that day and nothing is done only once; the Everyman first, in the original box; the Gambler second; the ZIP file is a browsable, in-story surface whose entries become readable on a schedule; the manual's pages are entries in it.*

## Two tracks, one calendar

**The machine track** is what a visitor can newly *do* that day. **The ZIP track** is what newly becomes *readable* in the browsable ZIP that day. They are the same story told from two sides: a feature arrives as a delivery (the inbox says *New Feature Added*), and the file that carried it appears in the ZIP where a visitor can go back and find it. The ZIP therefore structures the whole drip: its folders are the categories, its entries are the days, and what is still password-protected is what is still to come.

**The ZIP as the Record has already published it** (Records 002 to 005):

```
ROOT
 /(many pwd protected folders)
 MGK-VIIIp/MANUAL/  00-FRONTMATTER.tif · 07-POWER-SYSTEM.tif · 11-VID-LINK.tif · 31-PARITY-BIAS.tif
 PERSONNEL/         CEO/ (one page, redacted to the letterhead) · INFORMER/ (photographs only) · EVERYDAY/ (not yet opened) · GAMBLER/ (not yet opened)
 PORTAL/            TERMINAL.EXE (quarantined; it is the Portal) · PORTAL_2v16.CFG · /ANTENNA (PWD) · /CHANNEL_SELECT (PWD) · CH3-STANDARD/ · CH4-DETAIL/ · /INSTALL/QC_101.TIF
```

Everything below the line "many pwd protected folders" is the room the schedule lives in. Each week cracks one more folder. The folders proposed, in the order they crack: `ENGINES/` (the three acts) · `PROGRAMS/` (games, detectors, apps) · `PREFERENCES/` (polarity, clarity, voices) · `AUDIO/` (the sounds) · `USER/` (the name, the record) · and the rest of `MGK-VIIIp/MANUAL/` and `PERSONNEL/`, which open a page or a folder at a time.

**The ZIP browser is a build.** It does not exist. It is a new in-story surface (a file tree a visitor clicks through; no download; entries render as the plates and documents the Record already knows how to show). Until it is built, the Record's text narrates each recovery exactly as it has so far, and the browser catches up. Its brief comes after Sunday.

Legend: **first** · **update** · **dump** · **artifact** · **micro** (a small drop from a character on someone else's day) · ✦ one of the week's two-or-three real reveals · ⚑ needs something from Mike first (photographs, words, a ruling) · 🔧 needs a build.
