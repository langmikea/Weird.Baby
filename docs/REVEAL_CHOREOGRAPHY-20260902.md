# THE REVEAL CHOREOGRAPHY — first pass, 2026-09-02

**A proposal for Mike, not a landing.** Nothing here is written into the reveal ledger's `when` column until he says so. It places the device's important menu items, programs, adjustments, manual portions and artifacts across weeks two to twelve, one Record a weekday at 17:00, under his rule of 2026-09-02: *the story explains the device; every feature can be a day; things come out in phases with later updates; how it works is never itself a day.*

**How the phases work in-story.** The Parcel Law and the Downloads Fiction already give the mechanism: capability *arrives* as a transmission, and the inbox's text message **New Feature Added** is the reveal channel. So a "first appearance" day is a delivery; an "update" day is a second delivery that widens the same door. The Evolution Law fixes one order: **NIAC first, then v2.0, then 65** — the answer engines arrive in three acts, never side by side. The Bouncy Ball Law caps focus: **two or three real reveals a week; dumps have no ceiling.** The Never-Advertised Law keeps the eggs out of the schedule: they are found, never announced.

**How "how it works" is handled.** The controls (dial moves, shutter chooses; shake, scroll, click) and the way to get a determination are never a day. They ride as a manual dump under the first machine day, so a visitor who wants to know can read it and a visitor who wants to play does not have to.

**What this pass obeys that is already fixed:** week one as published (Records 001–005; the Portal goes public Friday 09-11); the arc's headlines for weeks 3, 4, 5, 9, 10, 11, 12; the promises ledger P1–P18 (the four personnel folders come due in week 3).

Legend: **first** = a feature's first appearance · **update** = a later delivery that widens it · **dump** = manual pages or documents riding under a day · **artifact** = a photographed object · ✦ = a real reveal for the week's two-or-three · ⚑ = needs something from Mike before it can post.
