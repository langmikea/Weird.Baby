# THE REVEAL CHOREOGRAPHY — second cut, 2026-09-02

**A proposal for Mike, not a landing.** Nothing here is written into the reveal ledger's `when` column until he says so. It places the device's important menu items, programs, adjustments, manual portions and artifacts across weeks two to twelve, one Record a weekday at 17:00, under his rule of 2026-09-02: *the story explains the device; every feature can be a day; things come out in phases with later updates; how it works is never itself a day.*

**Second cut, same day, on his direction:** one character a week, weeks two to five, each with a full day; the first on the Friday of week two. And a general rule, his words: *"Just because something is the feature for the day does not mean that nothing else may be done that day nor does it mean that thing may not be done more in the future."* Characters dress other days with micro-drops.

**How the phases work in-story.** The Parcel Law and the Downloads Fiction give the mechanism: capability *arrives* as a transmission, and the inbox's text message **New Feature Added** is the reveal channel. A "first" day delivers; an "update" day widens the same door. The Evolution Law fixes one order: **NIAC first, then v2.0, then 65.** The Bouncy Ball Law caps focus: **two or three real reveals a week; dumps and micro-drops have no ceiling.** The Never-Advertised Law keeps the eggs out of the schedule.

**How "how it works" is handled.** The controls and the way to get a determination ride as a manual dump under the first machine day. Never a day of their own.

Legend: **first** · **update** · **dump** (manual pages riding under a day) · **artifact** (a photographed object) · **micro** (a small drop from a character, dressing a day whose feature is something else) · ✦ = one of the week's real reveals · ⚑ = needs something from Mike first.
