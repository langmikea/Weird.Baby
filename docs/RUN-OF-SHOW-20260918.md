# THE RUN OF SHOW — three rough shapes, to point at

Ops, 2026-09-18. Range first (PLAN-20260918-LAUNCH.md section 2): these are
the limits, drawn fast, on what is already ruled. Nothing here is built or
scheduled. Point at one and say "something like that"; the chosen shape is
then made exact and every reel line is sized to fill it.

What is fixed, whichever shape: nothing posts before the door (no teasers,
ruled 09-17). The door opens Fri 10-30 at five. The Q&A posts every day at
five from Sat 10-31. The Number posts weekdays at noon from Mon 11-09
(moved 09-18), Coconuts for three weeks to Fri 11-27. Every caption points
at one room. The hot Q&A fires whenever you say "hot".

Where each lands:

```

THE NUMBER   the song's page on /wb

## A. Quiet: the two ruled lines and nothing else

```
Fri 10-30   5:00  the door opens. The ask reel, once, pinned: "what is this"
10-31 on    5:00  Q&A, daily
11-09 on   12:00  the Number, weekdays

One post a day for nine days, then two on weekdays. The least to make, the
least to go wrong; the albums get no push of their own.
