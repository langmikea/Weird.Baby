## 1. The rule the pieces follow

Mike's ruling of 09-03 (Q3, C "but more than a week"): one song at a time, in
pieces across its run: **the hook, a verse, the bridge, the story, the hook
again**, Monday to Friday. A song runs a floor of two weeks and a ceiling of
four while its sends and saves climb; Ops calls the week on Sundays. Fifteen
pieces are three weeks of five.

The song has no bridge on the sheet (verse, chorus, four times). Ops' call:
the third verse ("as we get old, or so I'm told, the bonds of love remain in
our control") fills the bridge slot; it is the one verse that turns the song
from the joke to the point. The hook is verse one into the first chorus (the
chorus is the hook proper: "I can't think of anything I'd rather do"). The
hook again is the last verse ("you are enough") into the last chorus and the
wave, so the week ends on the song's own ending.

## 4. What the tape lacks: the 10-20 sitting

The tape has three performances and no telling. The three story slots are the
sitting's, about 30 seconds each, to camera, the DJI pair on the voice:

1. **Who the song was written for.** Coconuts was written 2025-03-13 for
   Carina (the song file calls it "A Song for Carina"). Who she is to you and
   where the word came from.
2. **The chair, the egg, the bread.** What the line means: the small chain of
   doing things for someone.
3. **You are enough.** The last verse, and who it is said to.

Two Bs are cut from the tape's own talk so the slot can be judged on a reel,
not a transcript: after take 3 ("couple of screw-ups, but that's for reals,
man... that's how we play", 12 s) and after take 2 ("Jesse does it under the
power lines... I'd do it on the front porch, with the landscaping trucks and
the cicadas", 20 s). They are the porch's story, not the song's; Ops
recommends the sitting for all three and keeps the Bs for the weeks the
sitting does not cover.

Two spares, if the sitting has room (the task allows five): the hook shot
close (the DJI pair on the voice, the iPhone on the tripod at half the
distance), one for Monday 11-02 and one for Monday 11-09, so each week opens
on a fresh frame. The platforms throttle a repeated frame; three weeks in one
chair is the risk the spares answer. Not cut, not needed for the launch week.

## 6. What the line does to a piece (Ops' calls, not taste)

- **Lifted frame-accurately** from the tape (a re-encode, so the cut lands on
  the frame), then the reel line's own build: 1080x1920, 30 fps, the pop first.
  Nothing burned in.
- **Levelled.** The takes sit at -24 LUFS integrated (the Audigo's own gain);
  the pop is -21; the platforms play at about -14. Each piece is measured and
  lifted by one fixed gain to -16 LUFS with a true-peak limiter at -1.5 dBTP,
  so the performance's dynamics are untouched and every piece lands at the
  same level. The six pieces cut on 09-17 afternoon into reels › out › material
  were not levelled; these supersede them.
- **Eased at the edges,** sound only: 0.2 s in, 0.5 s out. A piece is lifted
  out of a running take; the picture cuts.
