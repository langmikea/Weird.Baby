## THE ARITHMETIC ONE RULE PERFORMS, SAID OUT LOUD

**PATIENCE TAKES THE TOP OF READING 5's RANGE, NOT ITS MIDDLE.** The reading is
*20–30*; the rule is **30**. That is a deliberate choice and not a rounding:
**a judgement made at 20 is a judgement made where the reading still permits
noise.** If reading 5 is re-taken and moves, this rule moves with it.

**CADENCE TAKES THE BOTTOM OF READING 1's RANGE.** The reading is *5–7*; the
rule is **5**, on weekdays — the lowest cadence the reading supports, which is
also the one a person can actually keep. **Five weekdays and five posts is the
same fact twice**, and it is why TIME has something to be the same as.

**Nothing else here computes anything**, and `release/` rule 4 stands: nothing
in this system derives a number from two readings.
